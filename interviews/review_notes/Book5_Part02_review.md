## Findings kept in the text (observed)
A sliding window is wrong with negatives: the naive window failed 401 of 1,500 seeded cases with negatives and none without (Q34, with a prefix-sums-plus-`bisect` reference); forgetting to delete a zero-count key makes `len(count)` stay high and `left` run off the end (Q24, Q25 deliberate buggy demos print IndexError); a stale `max_f` is fine for the longest-window variant but must be shown against the exact version (Q11); advancing `mid` after the swap with `high`, and skipping only the left side on duplicates, are the classic bugs (Q13, Q20); random data can yield two equally close sums so the cross-check compares distance, not the sum (Q27); the unique-pair promise in Q2 means its cross-check keeps only inputs with exactly one pair.
