Findings from the runs: all 720 sliding-puzzle boards agree with a backwards BFS table, 360 solvable, hardest 21 moves; finalise-on-first-pop Dijkstra is wrong on 2897 three-node graphs (weights -3..3, no negative cycle) and never with non-negative weights, the stale-check variant matches Bellman-Ford on 1500 random graphs; lazy Dijkstra pushes <= E + 1 and stale pops = pushes - reached; minimum height trees have 1 or 2 roots on all 18248 labelled trees with 2 to 7 nodes; in-place Bellman-Ford and visited-set Dijkstra are wrong for cheapest flights on 22 and 35 of 3000 seeded inputs; ordinary BFS is wrong for 0-1 grids on 803 of 2000; mark-on-push DFS order differs from recursive preorder on 367 of 800; a recursive DFS on a 5000-node path raises RecursionError at limit 1000 while iterative versions handle 2*10^5-node paths, a 300 x 300 grid and 10^5-node random graphs.
