## AI-era notes
Several 2026 prep pages treat "how do you use AI tools and tell me about a time one was wrong" as a standard prompt; Q17, Q22 and Q44 cover it. Policy claims are not made. Wave B should note that follow-up depth exposes rehearsed or AI-written stories and that candidates should not use AI to write their story (hard rule 7).
