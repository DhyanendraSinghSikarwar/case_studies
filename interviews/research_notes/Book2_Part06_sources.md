## AI-era notes
- RAG has become the default design-round topic for applied-AI and ML-platform roles; interviewers grade the retrieval stage more than generation and expect the candidate to scope the corpus first (document count, size, update rate, PDFs with tables).
- Debugging ("right document exists but the answer is wrong") and evaluation split (retrieval vs generation) are the recurring probes (Q10, Q11, Q39).
- Reviewing LLM-written RAG code is a plausible AI-era round (Q43).
