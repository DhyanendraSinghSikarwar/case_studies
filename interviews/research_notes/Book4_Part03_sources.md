## Design decisions
- 40 Qs: 15 three-star, 16 two-star, 9 one-star. Each prompt is self-contained, names a failure scenario or an artifact to produce, and says up front what cannot be executed.
- Scope: Docker (Part 2), orchestrators (Part 5), serving frameworks (Part 6), CI/CD (Part 7), monitoring (Part 8), cloud services (Parts 10-11), cost design (Part 12) only as pointers.
- Helm is covered by 3 Qs (basics Q11, templating Q22, upgrade and rollback Q23), with autoscaling, debugging, GPU and KServe/Kubeflow/training spread across the rest.
