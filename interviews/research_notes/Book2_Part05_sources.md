## AI-era notes
- Post-training is a specialist track: coding rounds ask for a simplified DPO loss and PPO clipping, and design rounds ask for preference-data platforms and safety evaluation harnesses (Q6, Q12, Q38, Q39).
- Reasoning-model post-training (GRPO, RLVR, test-time compute) now sits next to preference optimisation (Q20, Q21).
- Application-side guardrails are separate from model alignment, and loops ask how to combine and measure them (Q25-Q29).
