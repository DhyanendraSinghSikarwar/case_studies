**Say this first (1 line):** Thank you sincerely; after careful thought I have decided not to take the role, and I would like to keep in touch.

**Structure:**
1. Decide first, then tell them promptly, ideally by phone or a short call, then follow up in writing.
2. Thank them for specific things (time, interviews, the team).
3. State the decision clearly in the first lines; do not make them guess.
4. Give a short, true reason, or none; never criticise them.
5. Offer to stay in touch and, if true, say what impressed you.

**Strong sample answer (spoken):** (illustrative; replace details with your own)
"Thank you for the offer and for all the time your team spent with me. It was a hard decision. I have decided to accept a different opportunity that fits my current priorities, so I will not be taking this role. I was genuinely impressed by [specific thing about the team or work], and I would be glad to stay in touch. Thank you again for how well the process was run. If it is helpful, I can send feedback on my experience."

**Weak version (and why it fails):** Ignoring emails until the offer expires, or a blunt "No, the other place pays better." Silence burns the relationship with the recruiter and the team, and complaints about pay or people read as unprofessional. Do not claim reasons that are not true.

**What the interviewer scores:**
- Courtesy and promptness.
- Clarity: the decision is unmistakable.
- No blame, no negotiation by accident.
- Maturity in leaving the door open.

**Make it yours:**
- Pick the one or two real things you liked about the company or team.
- Choose a short, honest reason you are comfortable giving, such as fit with your current priorities.
- Decide if you want to leave the door open for the future, and say so only if you mean it.
- Tell the recruiter before the deadline, not after.
- If you are still unsure, ask for time instead (see Q21) rather than declining and reversing.

**Script (copy and adapt):**
```
Hi [recruiter name],
Thank you for the offer for the [role] and for the time you and [names] gave me.
After careful thought, I have decided not to proceed. This was not an easy decision.
I was particularly impressed by [specific thing].
I would be glad to stay in touch, and I wish you and the team well.
Best regards, [your name]
```

**Follow-ups:**
- *Is there anything we could change?* "Thank you for asking. It is mostly [short, true reason]. I do not think there is a gap you need to fix."
- *Can we reconnect later?* "Yes, I would like that. I will reach out on [professional network]."

**Common mistakes:**
- Declining by silence.
- Over-explaining or criticising.
- Declining by text only after an in-person process, without a call or thanks.
- Using a decline to start a negotiation you do not mean.
- Burning the bridge with the hiring manager who backed you.
