**Say this first (1 line):** Yes, there was a gap: here is briefly what happened, what I did during it, and why I am ready now.

**Structure:** Keep it short, factual and forward-looking.
1. Name it plainly in one sentence: the reason (health, family, study, layoff, relocation, a break) at the level you are comfortable sharing. You do not owe private details.
2. What you did: courses, projects, freelance work, caring responsibilities, rest. Only what is true.
3. How you kept skills current, with one proof point.
4. Why you are ready now and what you want next.
5. Stop and hand the conversation back.

**Strong sample answer (spoken):**
*Illustrative; replace with your own details.*
"From [month] to [month] I took a break for [reason, in a sentence]. During that time I [one or two real activities, such as finishing a course, building a small project, or part-time work]. I kept my skills current by [specific example], and I'm now ready to return full time. What drew me to this role is [specific reason]. I'm happy to walk you through the project I built, if that's useful."

**Weak version (and why it fails):** "Um, it's a long story, I couldn't find anything, and it was a bad market." It is vague and apologetic, and it leaves a question mark. Making up activities is worse: a follow-up will expose it. State the truth briefly.

**What the interviewer scores:**
- Are you honest, composed and brief?
- Is your current skill level credible, with proof?
- Are you clear about your motivation to return?
- Do you avoid blaming others?

**Make it yours:**
- Write the true reason in one sentence, at a level of detail you are comfortable sharing.
- List one to three real things you did or learned in the period.
- Pick one piece of evidence (a project, a course with a result, a repository) that you can discuss in depth.
- Practise stopping after the answer.
- Do not ask AI to invent activities for a gap; follow-ups will test anything you claim.

**Script (copy and adapt):**
```
"Between [month, year] and [month, year] I [reason, one sentence]. During that time I [real activity or learning]. I'm ready to return because [reason]. For example, I recently [real proof point], which I'd be glad to discuss."
```

**Follow-ups:**
- "Were you looking during that time?" Answer honestly, and say what you were doing in search and learning.
- "Are you worried about being out of date?" Name the specific skills you refreshed and how.

**Common mistakes:**
- Over-apologising or oversharing.
- Inventing activities or dates.
- Blaming a past employer.
- Having no proof of current skills.
