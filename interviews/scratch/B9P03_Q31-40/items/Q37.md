**Say this first (1 line):** Fair question: each move had a reason, and here is what I learned and what I am looking for now.

**Structure:** Give one honest thread through the moves.
1. Acknowledge the pattern without defensiveness.
2. Give a short reason for each move, in plain terms (project ended, layoff, scope mismatch, a clear learning goal). Avoid blaming people.
3. Show what you delivered or learned in each stop, even briefly.
4. Explain what you changed in how you choose roles.
5. Say why this role is a place to stay and grow, with specifics.

**Strong sample answer (spoken):**
*Illustrative; replace with your own details.*
"I see why it looks that way. My first move was because [honest reason, such as the project ended]. The second was [honest reason], and I learned [skill]. Looking back, I made one of those choices too quickly, and now I check [how you evaluate role fit, such as the team's work, growth path and manager] before accepting. Here, what draws me is [specific reason], and I'm looking for a place where I can own [kind of work] for a longer stretch. In each role I delivered [one real result], and I'm glad to talk about any of them."

**Weak version (and why it fails):** "Every company was bad, and my managers did not understand me." It blames others, offers no learning, and suggests the same story could repeat here.

**What the interviewer scores:**
- Do you take ownership of your choices?
- Is the account consistent and honest?
- Did you deliver something in each role?
- Do you show commitment and a reason to stay?

**Make it yours:**
- Write one honest sentence for each move, then check they hang together.
- Name one decision you would make differently.
- Identify what you now look for before accepting a role.
- Link that to something specific in this company or team.
- Keep your account consistent with your resume and any references.

**Script (copy and adapt):**
```
"Each move had a reason: [reason one], then [reason two]. From it I learned [lesson]. Now I look for [what you check before accepting]. This role fits because [specific reason], and I'm looking to grow here over [period]."
```

**Follow-ups:**
- "How do we know you will stay?" Point to the specific reasons you chose this role and what you want to build; do not make promises you cannot keep.
- "Which move was a mistake?" Name one honestly and say what you changed.

**Common mistakes:**
- Blaming employers.
- Giving different reasons than your references might.
- Promising to stay forever.
- Skipping what you delivered at each stop.
