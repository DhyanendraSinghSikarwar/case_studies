**Say this first (1 line):** Answer honestly and briefly: yes or no, the stage, and that this role is a priority, without naming names or sharing details you do not need to.

**Structure:** Three-part answer [G, editor guidance].
1. State the truth in a sentence: "Yes, I am speaking with a couple of other teams" or "No, this is the only process at the moment."
2. Say the stage in general terms, only if useful: early conversations, an interview round, or an offer with a date.
3. Return to interest in this role and ask a helpful process question: their timeline.
Never invent offers, interviews or deadlines; the honest version works, and a false claim can fall apart on a check.

**Strong sample answer (spoken):** "Yes, I am in conversation with a couple of other companies, mostly at early stages. This role is high on my list because of [real reason, such as the data problem or the team]. I would like to understand your timeline, so I can plan around it. If anything changes on my side, for example if I receive an offer with a deadline, I will tell you promptly, so you are not surprised. How many steps are left in your process, and when do you expect to decide?" And if there are none: "No, this is the main one I am focused on right now."

**Weak version (and why it fails):** Either boasting ("I have three offers, you need to hurry") or hiding ("I am not talking to anyone") when it is not true. The first sounds like pressure; the second is a lie that can be found out.

**What the interviewer scores:**
- Are you honest and calm?
- Do you show real interest in this role?
- Do you protect other companies' confidential details?
- Do you ask about the timeline respectfully?
- Do you sound like a colleague, not a bargainer?

**Make it yours:**
- Write your true status in one sentence.
- Write your real reason for preferring this role.
- Decide how much detail you are comfortable sharing.
- Prepare the timeline question.
- Practise the "none right now" version too.

**Script (copy and adapt):**
```
Yes, I am speaking with [number] other companies, at [early / later] stages. This role is a strong fit because of [your real reason].
Could you tell me about your timeline? If I get an offer with a date, I will let you know early.
```

**Follow-ups:**
- Which companies? "I keep other companies' details private, as I would yours; I am happy to share the stage."
- Do you have an offer? "Answer truthfully: yes with a date, or not yet. If yes, say so plainly and ask about timing."
- Would you leave a process for this one? "Only say it if true; otherwise say you would decide after seeing the full picture."

**Common mistakes:**
- Inventing competition to add pressure.
- Naming and criticising other employers.
- Saying you are "only interested here" when untrue.
- AI-era note: do not use a tool to craft a bluff; a live follow-up will find it.
