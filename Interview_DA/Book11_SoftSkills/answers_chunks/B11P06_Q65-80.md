**STAR breakdown:** Situation: the decision. Task: what I wanted then. Action: what I do now. Result: [change and lesson].
**If you have no direct experience:** Use a smaller true decision. Say this only if it is true for you. Do not invent a failure or a regret to sound humble.
**Likely follow-ups:** 1) *Why did you not change earlier?* An honest reason, without excuses. 2) *Any other regret?* Have one more small one, or say none big.
**Common mistakes:** Blaming a boss or company; picking a trivial or fake regret; no lesson.
