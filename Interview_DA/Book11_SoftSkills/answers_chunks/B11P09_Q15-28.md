### Q16 ⭐⭐ 🟢 How do you use the whiteboard, shared screen or notepad in a virtual round so that your thinking is visible?

**What the interviewer is checking:** Can they follow your reasoning, not just your final answer?
**Say this in the interview:** "I write my plan first in a few lines, think aloud while I work, and check the result before I say I am done."
**Explain simply:**
- Interviewers cannot see your head; they see only what you write and say.
- A visible plan (3 to 5 bullets) shows structure even if the code or formula has a small error.

4. Build step by step, saying one line per step ("first I filter to this year").
5. Test with two or three rows or a quick total check; say what you expected.
6. Summarise: "This gives X; one edge case I would also check is blank departments."

**Common mistakes:** Typing in silence; narrating every letter; skipping the check at the end; not asking permission to use a notepad.

Decision: a tough but fair interview is normal; ongoing disrespect is a signal about the workplace, and leaving politely is your right.
**If you have no direct experience:** If you have not faced a hostile interviewer, say how you would handle it, or use a tough viva or a demanding stakeholder (only if true). Say this only if it is true for you.
**Likely follow-ups:** 1) *Would you still take the job?* Only after weighing the whole picture and the rest of the team. 2) *Would you complain?* Politely, with facts, to the recruiter or HR, after the interview.
**Common mistakes:** Matching rudeness; agreeing with everything; staying in an abusive interview out of fear; walking out angrily instead of politely.
