**Answer: 28th from the bottom.**

### Hard

**Q21.** Positions 1 (top) to 6.
1. Who can be 1st? Kiran is above Mohan and Pooja. Lata is below Mohan, Neha is below Lata. Omar is not 1st. So only Kiran can be 1st. Kiran is 1st.
2. Mohan and Lata form a block (Lata immediately below Mohan), with Neha somewhere below Lata.
3. Omar and Pooja are in places with exactly one between them, with Omar above: (2, 4), (3, 5) or (4, 6).
4. Try (2, 4): the free places are 3, 5, 6. The Mohan and Lata block needs two places in a row: 5 and 6. Then Neha has no place below Lata. Fail.
5. Try (3, 5): free places are 2, 4, 6, no two in a row. Fail.
6. Try (4, 6): free places are 2, 3, 5. Block at 2 and 3, Neha at 5 (below Lata). All clues hold.
Order: Kiran, Mohan, Lata, Omar, Neha, Pooja (checked by testing all 720 orders: only this one fits).

(c) Total = 3 + 3 + 2 + 2 = 10 (the last group of 2 is at the bottom).
**Answer: (a) 3; (b) 2; (c) 10 people.**

**Q28.** Possible points per game: 3, 1 or 0. Each team plays 3 matches.
1. Bears have 1 point: the only way is one draw and two losses (no wins).
2. Lions have 7 points in 3 matches: 3 + 3 + 1, so two wins and one draw.
3. Wolves have 5 points: 3 + 1 + 1, so one win and two draws.
4. Tigers have 3 points: either one win and two losses, or three draws. Count the draws: Lions 1, Bears 1, Wolves 2 give 4 draw-appearances; each drawn match gives 2 appearances, so the total must be even. If Tigers had three draws the total would be 7, which is odd. So Tigers have one win and two losses, no draws.
5. Wolves have two draws, but Tigers have none, so Wolves drew with Lions and Bears. That uses up Lions' one draw and Bears' one draw.
6. Lions' other two games are wins, against Tigers and Bears. Bears lost their other two games (to Lions and to Tigers). Wolves' win must be against the Tigers (their only match left). Tigers' single win is over Bears.
Results: Lions beat Tigers, Lions beat Bears, Lions drew Wolves, Tigers beat Bears, Wolves beat Tigers, Bears drew Wolves (checked by testing all 729 result combinations: only this one gives these points).
**Answer: The draws were Lions v Wolves and Bears v Wolves; the Tigers were beaten by the Lions and by the Wolves.**
