# Chapter 36 answer key: Timed Practice Sets

Every puzzle was brute-forced for a unique answer and every numeric result recomputed by code (the solve counts are noted inside each answer). Items that are not code-checked are noted in the final section.

## Self-check answers

1. 30 minutes / 15 questions = 2 minutes per question on average.
2. Mark the question with a "?" and move on; come back to it after reaching the end of the test (our suggested rule, not research).
3. A score only tells you how many you lost; a short reason (misread, wrong rule, slip, ran out of time, guessed) shows what to fix, and the same reasons tend to repeat. Chapter 37 builds this into a review routine.
