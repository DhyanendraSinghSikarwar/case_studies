# Chapter 36 answer key: Timed Practice Sets

Every puzzle (except the family tree M3-Q13) was brute-forced for a unique answer and every numeric result recomputed by code (the solve counts are noted inside each answer). Items that are not code-checked, and the acceptance rule for open-ended items, are noted in the final section.

## Self-check answers

1. 30 minutes / 15 questions = 2 minutes per question on average.
2. Mark the question with a "?" and move on; come back to it after reaching the end of the test (our suggested rule, not research).
3. A score only tells you how many you lost; a short reason (misread, wrong rule, slip, ran out of time, guessed) shows what to fix, and the same reasons tend to repeat. Chapter 37 builds this into a review routine.

Correct reasoning: 6 clerks working 4 hours is 24 clerk-hours for 120 files, so the same 120 files always need 24 clerk-hours of work. With 9 clerks: 24 / 9 = 2.67 hours, i.e. 2 hours 40 minutes. Sense check: 9 clerks are 1.5 times as many, so the time is 4 / 1.5 = 2.67 hours. Answer: the fault is multiplying instead of dividing; the right time is 2 hours 40 minutes.
