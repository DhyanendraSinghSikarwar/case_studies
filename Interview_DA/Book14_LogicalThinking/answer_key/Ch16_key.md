# Chapter 16 answer key: Seating Arrangements (Linear, Circular, Two-Row)

Every seating puzzle in this chapter (examples 1 to 4, scenario 1 and questions Q1 to Q6, Q8 to Q16, Q19, Q21, Q23 to Q28, Q30) was checked by trying every possible arrangement of the people in the seats and counting how many satisfy all the clues; each has exactly one valid arrangement (scenario 1 deliberately has several until its extra rules are added). Round-table puzzles were checked with one person's seat fixed, which is the same as ignoring rotations. Q20 and Q29 were counted by listing every arrangement by script. The queue and arithmetic questions (example 5, Q7, Q17, Q18, Q22, Q28's moves) were recomputed by script. Conventions are the ones in the chapter: rows face north (right = east), round tables face the centre (right = anticlockwise), top row faces south and bottom row faces north in two-row layouts.
