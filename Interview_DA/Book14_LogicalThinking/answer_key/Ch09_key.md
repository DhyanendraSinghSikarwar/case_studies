# Chapter 9 answer key: Number, Letter and Pattern Series

Letter positions used throughout: A=1, B=2, C=3, D=4, E=5, F=6, G=7, H=8, I=9, J=10, K=11, L=12, M=13, N=14, O=15, P=16, Q=17, R=18, S=19, T=20, U=21, V=22, W=23, X=24, Y=25, Z=26. After 26 the count wraps to 1.

## Self-check answers

1. **26.** Gaps: 5, 5, 5 (11-6, 16-11, 21-16). Constant gap, so add 5: 21 + 5 = 26.
2. **1215.** Multipliers: 15/5 = 3, 45/15 = 3, 135/45 = 3, 405/135 = 3. Constant multiplier 3: 405 x 3 = 1215.
3. **S.** G=7, J=10, M=13, P=16: gap +3 each time. 16 + 3 = 19 = S.

## Practice answers

**Q1. 44.** Gaps: 7, 7, 7, 7. Constant gap of +7. 37 + 7 = 44.

**Q2. 84.** Gaps: -9, -9, -9 (111-120, 102-111, 93-102). Subtract 9: 93 - 9 = 84.

**Q3. 768.** Gaps differ (9, 36, 144), so test multipliers: 12/3 = 4, 48/12 = 4, 192/48 = 4. Multiply by 4: 192 x 4 = 768.

**Q4. 9.** Multipliers: 243/729 = 1/3, 81/243 = 1/3, 27/81 = 1/3. Divide by 3: 27 / 3 = 9.

**Q5. 24.** Gaps: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 (4-3, 6-4, 9-6, 13-9, 18-13; they grow by 1). Next gap 6: 18 + 6 = 24.

**Q6. 35.** Gaps: 49-50 = -1, 47-49 = -2, 44-47 = -3, 40-44 = -4. The amount subtracted grows by 1 each step. Next gap -5: 40 - 5 = 35.

**Q7. A.** Positions: K=11, I=9, G=7, E=5, C=3. Gap -2 each time. 3 - 2 = 1 = A.

**Q8. D.** Positions: T=20, P=16, L=12, H=8. Gap -4. 8 - 4 = 4 = D.

**Q9. 28.** Gaps: 21 - 14 = 7 and 42 - 35 = 7. The known gaps are both 7, so ? = 21 + 7 = 28 and then 35 - 28 = 7 fits.

**Q10. 24.** Multipliers: 48/96 = 1/2; 12 to 6 is 1/2. Halve each time: 48 / 2 = 24, and 24 / 2 = 12 fits.

**Q11. K.** Positions: B=2, E=5, H=8, ?, N=14, Q=17. Gap +3. 8 + 3 = 11 = K, and 11 + 3 = 14 = N fits.

**Q12. 160.** Gap is +5 each visitor. The 12th term needs 11 steps from the first: 105 + 11 x 5 = 105 + 55 = 160. (Step count is position minus 1.)

**Q13. 17.** Counts 1, 3, 5, 7, 9: gap +2. Row 9 is 8 steps after row 1: 1 + 8 x 2 = 17.

**Q14. Square.** The repeating block is circle, square, square (length 3). 14 divided by 3 is 4 remainder 2, so the 14th shape is the 2nd shape in the block: square. (Check: positions 13 = circle, 14 = square, 15 = square.)

**Q15. 40.** Gaps: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 (odd numbers). Next gap 11: 29 + 11 = 40. (Check: the terms are 0, 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36 plus 4, so the 7th is 36 + 4 = 40.)

**Q16. 730.** Gaps and multipliers are not constant (gaps 6, 18, 54, 162; multipliers 2.5, 2.8, about 2.93, about 2.98). Try "multiply then adjust": 4 x 3 = 12, minus 2 = 10; 10 x 3 = 30, minus 2 = 28; 28 x 3 = 84, minus 2 = 82; 82 x 3 = 246, minus 2 = 244. Rule: x 3, then - 2. Next: 244 x 3 = 732, minus 2 = 730.

**Q17. 25.** Split into two tracks. Odd places: 5, 8, 11, 14 (+3). Even places: 40, 35, 30 (-5). The 8th term is on the even track, which continues 30 - 5 = 25.

**Q18. Q.** Positions: D=4, T=20, G=7, S=19, J=10, R=18, M=13, ?. Odd places: 4, 7, 10, 13 (+3: D, G, J, M). Even places: 20, 19, 18 (-1: T, S, R). The 8th term is on the even track: 18 - 1 = 17 = Q.

**Q19. 47.** Gaps are 3, 4, 7, 11, which are not constant and do not grow by a fixed amount. Test "each term is the sum of the two before": 4 + 7 = 11, 7 + 11 = 18, 11 + 18 = 29. Next: 18 + 29 = 47.

**Q20. 38.** Gaps: 3, 5, 7, 9 (odd numbers, growing by 2). Next gap 11: 27 + 11 = 38.

**Q21. 38.** Divide each term by 2: 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17. These are the prime numbers in order (skipping 2). The next prime after 17 is 19, and 19 x 2 = 38.

**Q22. 219.** Subtract 3 from each term: 1, 8, 27, 64, 125. These are cubes (1, 2, 3, 4, 5 each cubed). Next cube: 6 x 6 x 6 = 216. Add 3 back: 219.

**Q23. M.** Positions: N=14, Q=17, U=21, Z=26, then 26 + 6 = 32, wrap: 32 - 26 = 6 = F. Gaps: 3, 4, 5, 6 (grow by 1). Next gap 7: 6 + 7 = 13 = M.

**Q24. 28 should be 27.** Most terms go up by 7: 6, 13, 20, ?, 34, 41, 48. After 20 we expect 27, then 34 (27 + 7), 41, 48 all fit. The term 28 breaks the rule; it should be 27.

**Q25. 43 should be 42.** Gaps between the first five terms: 2, 5, 8, 11 (2 to 4, 4 to 9, 9 to 17, 17 to 28). The gaps grow by 3, so the next gap is 14: 28 + 14 = 42, and then 42 + 17 = 59 fits (the last term). So 43 is wrong and should be 42.

**Q26. 60.** Gaps: 5, 6, 7, 8 (grow by 1). Next gap 9: 51 + 9 = 60.

**Q27. 41.** Sticks: 5, 9, 13, 17: gap +4 per added house. For 10 houses we add 9 more houses to the first: 5 + 9 x 4 = 41. (Check with a rule: sticks = 4 x houses + 1; 4 x 10 + 1 = 41.)

**Q28. 54.** Look at pairs of steps: 16 to 8 (divide by 2), 8 to 24 (multiply by 3), 24 to 12 (divide by 2), 12 to 36 (multiply by 3), 36 to 18 (divide by 2). The operations take turns: divide by 2, multiply by 3. The next one is multiply by 3: 18 x 3 = 54.

**Q29. 91.** Gaps: 4, 9, 16, 25 (squares of 2, 3, 4, 5). Next gap is 6 squared = 36: 55 + 36 = 91.

**Q30. 122.** Gaps: 1, 3, 9, 27 (each gap is 3 times the one before). Next gap 81: 41 + 81 = 122.

**Q31. 2520.** Multipliers: 3/1 = 3, 12/3 = 4, 60/12 = 5, 360/60 = 6 (the multiplier grows by 1). Next multiplier 7: 360 x 7 = 2520.

**Q32. 114.** Operations take turns: 6 x 3 = 18, 18 - 4 = 14, 14 x 3 = 42, 42 - 4 = 38. Rule: multiply by 3, then subtract 4. Next is multiply by 3: 38 x 3 = 114.

**Q33. 70.** Two tracks. Odd places: 3, 6, 12, 24 (doubles). Even places: 100, 90, 80 (-10). The 8th term is on the even track: 80 - 10 = 70.

**Q34. FPS.** Treat each position inside the group as its own series. First letters: B, C, D, E (positions 2, 3, 4, 5; +1) so next is 6 = F. Second letters: D, G, J, M (4, 7, 10, 13; +3) so next is 16 = P. Third letters: K, M, O, Q (11, 13, 15, 17; +2) so next is 19 = S. Answer: FPS.

**Q35. 40 should be 39.** Test "each term is the sum of the two before": 6 + 9 = 15, 9 + 15 = 24, 15 + 24 = 39. The series shows 40, so 40 is the odd one. Check with 39: 24 + 39 = 63 (matches), 39 + 63 = 102 (matches).

**Q36. 49.** Two rules by position (place). Odd places: 1, 9, 25, which are 1 x 1, 3 x 3, 5 x 5 (squares of odd numbers). Even places: 8, 64, 216, which are 2 cubed, 4 cubed, 6 cubed. The 7th term is on an odd place: the squares were of 1, 3, 5, so the next is 7 x 7 = 49. (Equivalent: place n odd gives n squared; place n even gives n cubed.)

**Q37. 1944.** Gaps and multipliers are not simple (multipliers 1.5, 2, 3, 6). Test "multiply the two before": 2 x 3 = 6, 3 x 6 = 18, 6 x 18 = 108. Next: 18 x 108 = 1944 (18 x 100 = 1800, 18 x 8 = 144, total 1944).

**Q38. 61.** Test "add the three before": 2 + 3 + 5 = 10, 3 + 5 + 10 = 18, 5 + 10 + 18 = 33. Next: 10 + 18 + 33 = 61.

**Q39. Z.** Positions: Y=25, X=24, U=21, P=16, I=9. Gaps: -1, -3, -5, -7 (odd numbers growing by 2). Next gap -9: 9 - 9 = 0, and position 0 wraps to 26 = Z.

**Q40. Rs 185.** Daily savings go up by Rs 3: 5, 8, 11, ... Day 10 saving is 5 + 9 x 3 = 32. List: 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26, 29, 32. Total: pair first and last (5 + 32 = 37), second and second last (8 + 29 = 37), 11 + 26 = 37, 14 + 23 = 37, 17 + 20 = 37. That is 5 pairs of 37: 5 x 37 = 185. Check by adding in order: 5+8=13, +11=24, +14=38, +17=55, +20=75, +23=98, +26=124, +29=153, +32=185.

## Verification note

Every question in this chapter was checked by computer against a library of rule types (constant gap, constant multiplier, changing gaps, changing multipliers, two interleaved tracks, squares, cubes, primes, add-the-two-before, add-the-three-before, product of the two before, alternating operations, repeating cycles, and wrap-around arithmetic for letters). In every case only one next value fitted. The three "wrong term" questions (24, 25, 35) were also checked: exactly one single-term correction repairs each list. The letter groups (34) were checked one position at a time. Q9 to Q11 (missing middle term) were checked by trying every candidate value. Q12 to Q14, Q27 and Q40 are word problems built on the same number series; their series were checked in the same way. Q36 (squares on odd places, cubes on even places) relies on a rule by place number, so it is unique only within the families listed above.
