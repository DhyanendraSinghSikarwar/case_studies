## Practice answers

**Q1** (a) Collect every name once: {Asha, Bilal, Chen, Dara, Esha, Farid, Gita}, 7 people. (b) Names in both lists: {Chen, Esha}. (c) X without the overlap: {Asha, Bilal, Dara}. (d) Y without the overlap: {Farid, Gita}. Check: 3 + 2 + 2 = 7.

**Q2** At least one = 25 + 18 - 6 = 37 (the 6 were counted twice). Neither = 40 - 37 = 3.

**Q3** (a) True: every passer is in the batch. (b) False: 8 batch members did not pass. (c) True: A is inside S, so the overlap is all of A, which is 22. (d) True: the union is just S, 30 people.

**Q4** (a) 30 - 12 = 18. (b) Remote 12, of whom 2 are on leave: 12 - 2 = 10. (c) Remote or on leave = 12 + 5 - 2 = 15; neither = 30 - 15 = 15.

**Q5** (a) Only A = 60 - 25 = 35. (b) Only B = 45 - 25 = 20. (c) 60 + 45 - 25 = 80 (check 35 + 25 + 20 = 80).

**Q6** (a) 14 + 9 + 11 + 6 = 40. (b) A = only A + both = 14 + 9 = 23. (c) B = 11 + 9 = 20. (d) Exactly one = 14 + 11 = 25.

**Q7** (a) 8. (b) Not in A = only B + neither = 12 + 15 = 27. (c) At most one = everything except both = 8 + 12 + 15 = 35. (d) 12.

**Q8** The monthly circle sits entirely inside the weekly circle. (a) The union is the weekly set: 70. (b) Weekly only = 70 - 30 = 40. (c) Both = 30 (all monthly subscribers).

**Q9** At least one = 120 - 20 = 100. Both = 72 + 65 - 100 = 37. Only phone = 72 - 37 = 35. Only laptop = 65 - 37 = 28. Check 35 + 37 + 28 + 20 = 120.

**Q10** Only yoga = 30 - 12 = 18; only swim = 28 - 12 = 16; exactly one = 34. At least one = 30 + 28 - 12 = 46; neither = 50 - 46 = 4. Check 34 + 12 + 4 = 50.

**Q11** Both = 55 + 50 - 85 = 20. Only B = 50 - 20 = 30. Neither = 100 - 85 = 15. Check: only A 35, both 20, only B 30, neither 15 = 100.

**Q12** Let neither = n, so both = 2n. At least one = 90 - n. Also at least one = 54 + 48 - 2n = 102 - 2n. So 90 - n = 102 - 2n, giving n = 12 and both = 24. Check: at least one = 78 = 54 + 48 - 24, and 78 + 12 = 90.

**Q13** At least one = 50 + 40 + 30 - 15 - 12 - 10 + 6 = 89. None = 100 - 89 = 11. Only E = 50 - 15 - 12 + 6 = 29; only S = 40 - 15 - 10 + 6 = 21; only P = 30 - 12 - 10 + 6 = 14; exactly one = 64. Exactly two = (15 - 6) + (12 - 6) + (10 - 6) = 9 + 6 + 4 = 19. Check 64 + 19 + 6 = 89.

**Q14** Total = 10 + 8 + 6 + 5 + 4 + 3 + 2 + 7 = 45. A = 10 + 5 + 4 + 2 = 21. B = 8 + 5 + 3 + 2 = 18. C = 6 + 4 + 3 + 2 = 15. At least two = 5 + 4 + 3 + 2 = 14.

**Q15** The pair totals include the centre, so remove it. A and B only = 14 - 5 = 9; B and C only = 11 - 5 = 6; A and C only = 9 - 5 = 4. Exactly two = 19. At least two = 19 + 5 = 24.

**Q16** (i) Can be correct: at least one = 25 + 22 - 9 = 38, which is at most 40; 2 people use neither. (ii) Cannot: at least one = 30 + 35 - 10 = 55, more than the 50 people. (iii) Cannot: the "both" group (25) cannot be larger than B (20), because everyone in both is in B.

**Q17** (a) If nobody is in neither, at least one = 100. Both = 70 + 60 - 100 = 30. (b) At least one cannot exceed 100, so both = 130 - (at least one) is at least 30. Both cannot exceed the smaller circle (60), and 60 is reachable (all of B also in A, then at least one = 70 which fits). Smallest 30, largest 60.

**Q18** Exactly one = (120 - x) + (90 - x) = 210 - 2x, where x = both. So 210 - 2x = 130, giving x = 40. At least one = 210 - 40 = 170; neither = 200 - 170 = 30. Check: only bread 80, only milk 50, both 40, neither 30 = 200.

**Q19** At least one = 120 - 10 = 110. Let x = all three. 60 + 50 + 55 - 20 - 25 - 18 + x = 110, so 102 + x = 110 and x = 8. Only Excel = 55 - 25 - 18 + 8 = 20. Only Python = 60 - 20 - 25 + 8 = 23; only SQL = 50 - 20 - 18 + 8 = 20. Exactly one = 23 + 20 + 20 = 63. Check: exactly two = (25 - 8) + (20 - 8) + (18 - 8) = 17 + 12 + 10 = 39; 63 + 39 + 8 = 110. Brute force over all region fillings gives only x = 8.

**Q20** Discounted or out of stock = discounted (36) + out-of-stock-not-discounted (4) = 40. Out of stock and discounted = 24 - 4 = 20. Neither = 60 - 40 = 20. Check: discounted only 16, both 20, out of stock only 4, neither 20 = 60.

**Q21** Sum of circle totals = 70 + 60 + 50 = 180. 180 = (exactly one) + 2 x 40 + 3 x 10 = (exactly one) + 110. So exactly one = 70. Check: at least one = 70 + 40 + 10 = 120, and none = 150 - 120 = 30, as given. Brute force confirms 70 is the only possible value.

**Q22** Count the people who are missing each item: 100 - 80 = 20 miss A, 100 - 75 = 25 miss B, 100 - 70 = 30 miss C. Anyone who misses at least one item is on at least one of these lists, so at most 20 + 25 + 30 = 75 people miss something (this is the largest it can be, when no one misses two items). So at least 100 - 75 = 25 people own all three. Largest: everyone who owns all three is inside the smallest circle (C), so at most 70 (the size of C); 70 is reachable when everyone who owns C also owns A and B. Smallest 25, largest 70 (confirmed by enumerating every region filling).

**Q23** A ∪ B = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8}, so (A ∪ B)' = {9, 10, 11, 12}. A' = {7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12}; B' = {1, 2, 3, 9, 10, 11, 12}; A' ∩ B' = {9, 10, 11, 12}. A ∩ B = {4, 5, 6}, so (A ∩ B)' = {1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12}. A' ∪ B' = {1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12}. Equal pairs: (A ∪ B)' = A' ∩ B', and (A ∩ B)' = A' ∪ B' (De Morgan's rules).

**Q24** Only pool = 40 - 14 - 12 + 5 = 19. Only floor = 36 - 14 - 10 + 5 = 17. Only classes = 30 - 10 - 12 + 5 = 13. Exactly two = (14 - 5) + (10 - 5) + (12 - 5) = 9 + 5 + 7 = 21. At least one = 19 + 17 + 13 + 21 + 5 = 75 (formula: 40 + 36 + 30 - 14 - 10 - 12 + 5 = 75). None = 90 - 75 = 15.

**Q25** Sum of club sizes = 40 + 35 + 25 = 100. Let a = exactly one club, c = all three. a + 14 + c = 80, so a + c = 66. Also a + 2 x 14 + 3c = 100, so a + 3c = 72. Subtract: 2c = 6, so c = 3 and a = 63. Brute force over all region fillings confirms c = 3 and a = 63 for every valid filling.
