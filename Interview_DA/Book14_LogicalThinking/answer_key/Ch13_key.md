# Chapter 13 Answer Key: Direction and Distance

Notation: position is (across, up) from the start (0, 0); east and north are plus, west and south are minus. Compass order clockwise: N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW (45 degrees each).

## Self-check answers

1. **East.** A right turn from north is a quarter turn clockwise, which gives east.
2. **0 km.** 5 km north takes you to (0, 5); 5 km south brings you back to (0, 0). You walked 10 km but are at the start.
3. Distance travelled is the sum of every leg walked; distance from the start is the straight line between start and end, which can be much smaller.

## Practice answers

**Q1** Position: 9 east then 12 north gives (9, 12). Straight line: 9 and 12 are 3 times 3 and 4, so 3 times 5 = 15 km. Across plus, up plus. **15 km, north-east of the depot.**

**Q2** East, turn right (cw) gives south; turn right again gives west. **West.**

**Q3** North-west is one step before north in clockwise order. A 90 degree turn is two steps cw: NW to N to NE. **North-east.**

**Q4** Put G at (0, 0). S is 15 m west: (-15, 0). O is 20 m south of S: (-15, -20). Across 15, up 20 is 5 times (3, 4), so 5 times 5 = 25 m. Both minus. **25 m, south-west of G.**

**Q5** Start facing north: 7 north gives (0, 7). Right turn from north faces east: 3 east gives (3, 7). Right turn from east faces south: 7 south gives (3, 0). She is 3 km from the start, due east of it. Walked: 7 + 3 + 7 = 17 km. **3 km, due east; walked 17 km.**

**Q6** Morning: sun in the east, shadow falls west. Shadow is on her left, so her left is west. Facing north, left is west. **North.**

**Q7** Facing south, walk 10 m: (0, -10). Left turn from south faces east: 5 m gives (5, -10). The mailroom (0, 0) is 5 m west and 10 m north of him: **north-west of him**. Walked 10 + 5 = **15 m**.

**Q8** Anticlockwise order from south: S, SE, E. One step (45 degrees) acw from south is south-east. **South-east.**

**Q9** Y at (0, 0), X at (20, 0), Z at (0, 15). From X to Z: 20 west and 15 north. 20 and 15 are 5 times 4 and 3, so 5 times 5 = 25 km. Z is to the west and north of X. **25 km, Z is north-west of X.**

**Q10** Asha at (0, 11), Babu at (0, -5). Both on the same north-south line: 11 + 5 = 16 km apart. Asha is further north. **16 km, Asha is due north of Babu.**

**Q11** Facing north, 14 km: (0, 14). Right gives east, 16 km: (16, 14). Right from east gives south, 26 km: (16, -12). Straight line: 16 and 12 are 4 times 4 and 3, so 4 times 5 = 20 km. Across plus, up minus. **20 km, south-east of the start.**

**Q12** Facing west, 8 km: (-8, 0). Left from west gives south, 15 km: (-8, -15). Left from south gives east, 8 km: (0, -15). Across is zero, so she is 15 km due south of home. Walked: 8 + 15 + 8 = 31 km. **15 km due south of home; walked 31 km.**

**Q13** Use bearings in degrees clockwise from north: NE = 45. Plus 135 gives 180. Minus 270 (anticlockwise) gives -90, which equals 270 (west). Plus 45 gives 315. 315 is north-west. **North-west.**

**Q14** Evening: sun in the west, shadow falls east. Shadow on his left, so his left is east. Facing south, left is east. **South.**

**Q15** Gita: 18 km/h for 1.5 hours = 27 km north: (0, 27). Hari: 18 km/h for 2 hours = 36 km west: (-36, 0). Difference: 36 across, 27 up; 36 and 27 are 9 times 4 and 3, so 9 times 5 = 45 km. Hari is west and south of Gita. **45 km apart, Hari is south-west of Gita.**

**Q16** Facing NE (45 degrees). Left is a quarter turn anticlockwise: 45 minus 90 = -45, which is 315, north-west. Behind is a half turn: 45 + 180 = 225, south-west. **Omar is north-west of Neha; Pia is south-west of Neha.**

**Q17** Walked: 8 + 15 = 23 km. Position (-8, -15); straight line: 8 and 15 give 17 (friendly triple 8, 15, 17). Difference 23 - 17 = **6 km shorter**.

**Q18** Bearing: SW = 225. 90 acw: 225 - 90 = 135 (SE). 135 cw: 135 + 135 = 270 (W). 225 acw: 270 - 225 = 45 (NE). **North-east.**

**Q19** Facing east, 6 km: (6, 0). Left from east faces north, 8 km: (6, 8). Left from north faces west, 14 km: (-8, 8). Right from west faces north, 7 km: (-8, 15). She now faces north. Straight line: 8 and 15 give 17 km. Across minus, up plus. **17 km, north-west of the start, facing north.**

**Q20** Anil: 16 north, 12 east: (12, 16). Straight line: 12 and 16 are 4 times 3 and 4, so 20 m. Beena: 9 west, 12 south: (-9, -12). 9 and 12 are 3 times 3 and 4, so 15 m. Beena is nearer by 20 - 15 = 5 m. Between them: across 12 - (-9) = 21, up 16 - (-12) = 28; 21 and 28 are 7 times 3 and 4, so 35 m. Anil is east and north of Beena. **Beena is nearer by 5 m; they are 35 m apart; Anil is north-east of Beena.**

**Q21** Facing north, 10 km: (0, 10). Right (east), 7 km: (7, 10). Left from east is north, 6 km: (7, 16). Left from north is west, 22 km: (-15, 16). Right from west is north, 4 km: (-15, 20). Facing north. Straight line: 15 and 20 are 5 times 3 and 4, so 25 km. Across minus, up plus. **25 km, north-west of the start, facing north.**

**Q22** After 12 km east she is at (12, 0). Left from east is north, so she ends at (12, d). Distance to depot: 12 squared plus d squared = 20 squared, so d squared = 400 - 144 = 256, d = 16 (distance cannot be negative). Total travelled: 12 + 16 = 28 km. She is east and north of the depot. **16 km after the turn; 28 km in total; north-east of the depot.**

**Q23** Morning: shadows fall west. Tara's shadow is in front of her, so Tara faces west. Back to back means Uday faces the opposite way: east. His shadow also falls west, which is behind him. Tara turns 90 degrees anticlockwise from west: west, then south. **Uday faces east, his shadow falls behind him; Tara walks south.**

**Q24** B (0, 0). A: 4 north gives (0, 4). C: 6 east of A gives (6, 4). D: 8 south of C gives (6, -4). E: 6 west of D gives (0, -4). E is 4 m due south of B. E to C: across 6, up 8 (4 + 4); 6 and 8 give 10. **E is 4 m due south of B; E is 10 m from C.**

**Q25** The 12 points north. At 4:30 the minute hand is on the 6, which points south. The hour hand is halfway between 4 and 5: 4.5 hours times 30 degrees = 135 degrees clockwise from north, which is south-east. Angle between south (180) and south-east (135) is 45 degrees. **Minute hand south, hour hand south-east, 45 degrees apart.**

**Q26** Bearings: east = 90. 90 acw: 90 - 90 = 0 (north). Some clockwise angle x: 0 + x = x. Then 45 acw: x - 45. This must equal south-west = 225, so x = 270. Check: 270 is less than 360, and no other value below 360 works. **270 degrees clockwise** (the same as a quarter turn anticlockwise).

**Q27** Leg 1: 24 minutes at 30 km/h = 0.4 hour times 30 = 12 km south: (0, -12). Right from south faces west. Leg 2: 40 minutes at 24 km/h = two thirds of an hour times 24 = 16 km west: (-16, -12). Straight back: 16 and 12 give 20 km. Time back: 20 km at 40 km/h = 0.5 hour = 30 minutes. Total: 24 + 40 + 30 = 94 minutes. The hub is east and north of him. **94 minutes (1 hour 34 minutes); the hub is north-east of the rider.**

**Q28** Legs 2, 5, 8, 11, 14, turning right after each one except the last, starting north. Headings: north, east, south, west, north.
- 2 north: (0, 2)
- 5 east: (5, 2)
- 8 south: (5, -6)
- 11 west: (-6, -6)
- 14 north: (-6, 8)

Straight line: 6 and 8 give 10 km. Across minus, up plus. **10 km, north-west of the start.**

**Q29** X (0, 12), Y (9, 0), Z (-16, 0).
- X to Y: across 9, up 12; 3 times (3, 4) gives 15 km.
- X to Z: across 16, up 12; 4 times (4, 3) gives 20 km.
- Y to Z: both on the east-west line: 9 + 16 = 25 km.

The largest is 25 km. **Y and Z are farthest apart at 25 km; X to Y is 15 km; X to Z is 20 km.**

**Q30** Work backward from the end. Final bearing 0 (north). Undo the last turn (45 acw): add 45, giving 45. Undo 90 cw: subtract 90, giving -45, which is 315. Undo 135 acw: add 135, giving 450, which is 90 (east). Check forward: 90 - 135 = -45 (315); +90 = 405, which is 45; -45 = 0, north. **East.**
