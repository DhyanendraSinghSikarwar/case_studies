# Chapter 14 Answer Key: Blood Relations and Family Trees

Conventions used throughout (as stated in the chapter): siblings are full siblings, each person has one marriage, nobody marries a blood relative, "husband" means a man and "wife" means a woman, and the family described is the whole family. Every chain answer below was checked by building the family in a small program and reading the relation off it. The multi-person puzzles (Q35, Q38, Q39) were checked by exhaustive search.

---

## Self-check answers

1. **Aunt** (your maternal uncle's wife). Path: mother, then her brother, then his wife. She is related to you by marriage, not by blood.
2. **No.** "A is the brother of B" tells you A is male and that A and B share parents. B could be a boy or a girl.
3. "Only" means **exactly one person**. "My father's brother" could be several men, so "his wife" would not be definite. "My father's only brother" is one man, so you can keep following the chain.

## Easy

**Q1.** Hana says, "Leo is the husband of my father's only sister."
1. Hana's father (M).
2. His only sister: Hana's paternal aunt (F).
3. Her husband is Leo.
4. The husband of a parent's sister is an uncle by marriage.

**Answer: Leo is Hana's uncle (her father's sister's husband).** Check: Hana is Leo's niece by marriage.

**Q2.** Neil's wife is Olga (F), Olga is the only daughter of Pavel, so Pavel is Olga's father. Quinn is Neil's son, so Quinn is also Olga's son (one marriage). Pavel is the father of Quinn's mother.

**Answer: Pavel is Quinn's maternal grandfather.**

**Q3.** Wen's wife is Xia. Xia's only sister is Yan, so Yan is the sister of Wen's wife.

**Answer: Yan is Wen's sister-in-law.**

**Q4.** Count by group: Rosa = 1; her two sons = 2; their two wives = 2; grandchildren: 2 couples × 2 children = 4. Total: 1 + 2 + 2 + 4 = **9 people.**

**Q5.** Joel is the son of Lena's brother. So Joel's father is Lena's brother, and Lena is the sister of Joel's father. A father's sister (Lena is a woman) is a paternal aunt.

**Answer: Lena is Joel's paternal aunt.**

**Q6.** Cole's mother has exactly two children, Cole and Dina, so Cole and Dina are siblings. Eli is Dina's husband (M), so Eli is the husband of Cole's sister.

**Answer: Eli is Cole's brother-in-law.** (This holds whether Cole is a boy or a girl.)

**Q7.** Zoya's mother's only daughter is a woman who is a daughter of Zoya's mother. Zoya is also a daughter of that mother (she is a woman), and "only daughter" means there is just one. So the "only daughter" is **Zoya herself**. The man is the husband of Zoya.

**Answer: He is Zoya's husband.**

**Q8.** Gus is the husband of Sam's father's mother (Sam's paternal grandmother). So Gus is the husband of Sam's paternal grandmother.

**Answer: Gus is Sam's paternal grandfather.**

**Q9.** A is a sister (A female). B is a son (B male) of C, and C is the wife of D, so D is the husband of B's mother, which is B's father. A is B's sister, so A shares the same father.

**Answer: D is A's father.**

**Q10.** Grandchildren: 2 + 2 + 1 = **5**. Descendants: 3 children + 5 grandchildren = **8**.

**Q11.** Aisha's husband is the son of Aisha's mother-in-law. If the mother-in-law has exactly one son, that son is Aisha's husband.

**Answer: He is Aisha's husband.**

**Q12.** Tess's mother (1), her mother (2), her mother (3): Tess's mother's mother's mother.

**Answer: Una is Tess's maternal great-grandmother.**

**Q13.** A man's brother-in-law can be: (1) his **wife's brother**; (2) his **sister's husband**; (3) his **wife's sister's husband** (the husband of his wife's sister). Three different people, all called brother-in-law. (For a woman, the same word also covers the **husband's brother**.)

**Q14.** Olive is Nils's wife, and Nils is Mia's husband's father. So Olive is Mia's husband's mother.

**Answer: Olive is Mia's mother-in-law.**

**Answer: The woman is Sana's husband's aunt (his mother's brother's wife), so Sana's aunt by marriage (aunt-in-law).** There is no single short English word for this, so describing the path is correct.

**Q20.** Ahmed is the father of Lina. Lina and Marco have a son, Nico, so Nico is Lina's son. Nico is the child of Ahmed's daughter.

**Q25.** Rosa's mother's only sister is Rosa's maternal aunt (F). Her husband is the aunt's husband, and his son is also the aunt's son (one marriage). The aunt is Rosa's mother's sister, so the son is the child of Rosa's mother's sibling.

**Q26.** Pim and Quin are siblings (children of Ola). Rae is Pim's daughter and Sid is Quin's son, so Rae and Sid are children of siblings. Ola is the mother of the parents of both.

**Q27.** Count by generation: 1 great-grandfather; 2 children; 4 grandchildren; 4 great-grandchildren (each of the 4 grandchildren has 1 child). Total people: 1 + 2 + 4 + 4 = **11**. **Q28.** Aidan is a man, so his mother's daughters are his sisters. His mother's son-in-law is the husband of one of those daughters, so the husband of Aidan's sister (only one). The girl's father is that man, so the girl is the daughter of Aidan's sister.

## Hard

**Q31.** Translate the chain P * Q - R / S * T one link at a time.
- P * Q: P is the mother of Q (P female).
- Q - R: Q is the sister of R (Q female; R is her sibling).
- R / S: R is the husband of S (R male, S female).
- S * T: S is the mother of T. T is the child of R and S (one marriage).

R is Q's sibling and male, so R is Q's brother and P's son (siblings share parents). R is T's father.

(a) P is the mother of R, and R is T's father, so P is **T's paternal grandmother**.
(b) Q is R's sister, and R is T's father, so Q is **T's paternal aunt**.

**Q32.** Translate X # Y @ Z $ W one link at a time.
- X # Y: X is the daughter of Y (X female).
- Y @ Z: Y is the brother of Z (Y male).
- Z $ W: Z is the father of W (Z male).

So Y and Z are brothers. X is Y's daughter and W is Z's child, so X and W are children of brothers.

**Answer: W is X's cousin.** Gender: **X is female** (daughter), **Y is male** (brother), **Z is male** (father), **W is not stated**, so W's gender cannot be stated.

**Q33.** Build the tree: Abel (M) is the father of Bina and Carl. Dena is Carl's wife, so Carl is male. Ezra (M) is the only son of Carl and Dena. Fia is Ezra's only sister, so Fia is also a child of Carl and Dena (F). Gil is Bina's husband, so Bina is female, Gil is male.

(a) Fia is Carl's daughter, so Abel's son's daughter: **Abel's granddaughter**.
(b) Gil is the husband of Bina, and Bina is Ezra's father's sister: **Gil is Ezra's uncle (his father's sister's husband)**.
(c) Dena is the wife of Bina's brother Carl: **Dena is Bina's sister-in-law**.
(d) Bina is the sister of Fia's father Carl: **Bina is Fia's paternal aunt**.

**Q35.** The smallest group has **4 people**.

Why 4 works. Let G be a woman with two daughters M and S, and let M have a daughter N.
```
        G
       / \
      M   S
      |
      N
```
- Grandmother: G (grandmother of N). That is 1.
- Mothers: G (mother of M and S) and M (mother of N). That is 2.
- Daughters: M, S and N. That is 3.
- Aunt: S (aunt of N). That is 1.
- Niece: N (niece of S). That is 1.
- Sisters: M and S are sisters of each other. That is 2.

Why fewer cannot work. An aunt and a niece need three people: the aunt, the niece, and the niece's parent who is the aunt's sister. The grandmother has to be two generations above the niece, so she cannot be any of those three (the aunt and the niece's mother are only one generation above the niece). So at least a fourth person is needed. A search over all small female-only family structures with 2 to 5 members found no solution with 2 or 3 people and solutions from 4 people.

**Q36.** Zed's father's mother is Zed's paternal grandmother. Her grandsons include Zed (a man, and a grandson of his father's mother). "The only grandson" means there is exactly one grandson, so it is **Zed himself**. The boy is the only son of Zed.

**Answer: The boy is Zed's son.**

**Q37.** Let s = sons and d = daughters, with s + d = 9.
- A daughter has d − 1 sisters and s brothers. "2 more brothers than sisters": s = (d − 1) + 2 = d + 1.
- A son has s − 1 brothers and d sisters. "As many brothers as sisters": s − 1 = d, so s = d + 1.

Both clues say the same thing: s = d + 1. With s + d = 9: (d + 1) + d = 9, so 2d = 8, d = 4, s = 5.

Check: a daughter has 3 sisters and 5 brothers (2 more brothers); a son has 4 brothers and 4 sisters (equal). Total 5 + 4 = 9. A search over all splits of 9 children confirmed this is the only one.

**Answer: 5 sons and 4 daughters.**

**Q38.**
1. Three generations with two people each. Pat's grandmother is Robin, so Robin is in the oldest generation and Pat in the youngest. Kim is Pat's sister, so Kim is also youngest, and female.
2. Jo is Kim's mother and Sam is Jo's husband. Parents and spouses are in the same generation, so Jo and Sam are the middle generation. That leaves Alex and Robin as the oldest generation.
3. Alex is Jo's father-in-law, so Alex is the father of Jo's husband Sam. Alex is male.
4. Jo cannot be a child of Alex and Robin (she would then be Sam's sister, and no one is married to a sibling). So Jo is married in from outside, and Sam's mother is the other person in the oldest generation: Robin (female, by "grandmother").
5. The youngest generation (Kim, Pat) are Sam and Jo's two children. Sam has exactly one son and one daughter. Kim is a daughter, so Pat is the son.

(a) **Robin** is Sam's mother.
(b) Pat is the son of Sam, and Sam is the son of Alex: **Pat is Alex's grandson (his son's son)**.
(c) Robin is Sam's mother and Sam is Jo's husband: **Robin is Jo's mother-in-law**.

A search over every possible family on these six people (genders and parent links) with these clues found exactly one family, matching the above.

**Q39.**
1. Three generations with two people each. Chris is the husband of Dana, so they are in the same generation. Dana is the grandmother of Gray and Harper, so Dana and Chris are the oldest, and Gray and Harper the youngest.
2. That leaves Eden and Frankie as the middle generation.
3. Frankie is the son-in-law of Chris: Frankie is male and married to a daughter of Chris. A son-in-law is not a blood relative, so Frankie is married in, and his wife is Chris's daughter in the middle generation: **Eden** (female).
4. Gray and Harper are siblings in the youngest generation, and their parents must be a couple from the middle generation: Frankie and Eden.

(a) **Gray's parents are Frankie and Eden.**
(b) **Eden is Chris's daughter.**
(c) **No.** Nothing says whether Gray is a boy or a girl. The search over all genders found four possibilities for Gray and Harper (boy/girl combinations), all consistent with the facts. So Gray could be Chris's grandson or granddaughter.

**Q40.** From statements 1 and 3: Ned is Olly's brother, so Ned and Olly share parents. Pia is Ned's sister, so Pia shares those same parents. That makes **Pia Olly's sister**. But statement 2 says Olly is Pia's father. A person cannot be both a sister and a daughter of the same man (and a father cannot share his own parents with his child). So **statements 1 and 3 together clash with statement 2**. All three cannot be true.

Repair: change one word in statement 3 so that Pia is Ned's **niece** (the daughter of his brother). Then: Ned is Olly's brother, Olly is Pia's father, Pia is Ned's niece. All three can be true: Olly is Pia's father and Ned is Pia's uncle.
