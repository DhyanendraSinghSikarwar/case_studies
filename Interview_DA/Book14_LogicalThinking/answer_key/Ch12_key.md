# Chapter 12 answer key: Syllogisms

Every answer below was checked by listing every possible Venn picture of the groups by computer (each group has at least one member; "some" means at least one). Hand-written reasoning is given alongside.

## Self-check answers

1. **No.** "All laptops are devices" puts laptops inside devices. Devices could include tablets or phones, so the reverse is not forced (a picture with a non-laptop device obeys the statement).
2. **No.** The chipped mugs and the cheap chipped items may be different items, so no mug need be cheap. Two "Some" statements do not join up.
3. **(b).** "Only managers can approve refunds" means everyone who can approve refunds is a manager. It does not say every manager can approve (some managers may not).

### Q1 🟢
**Answer: I and III follow.**
1. Draw one circle per group and translate each statement:
   - All coupons are offers. means: circle coupons lies inside circle offers.
   - All offers are discounts. means: circle offers lies inside circle discounts.
2. Draw the loosest picture the statements allow, then test each conclusion against every picture:
   - I. All coupons are discounts. FOLLOWS: true in all 8 possible pictures (checked by computer).
   - II. All discounts are coupons is only POSSIBLE. In one picture it is true (the picture holds: a member of coupons, offers and discounts only). In another it is false (the picture holds: a member of discounts only; a member of coupons, offers and discounts only). Both obey the statements, so it does not follow.
   - III. Some discounts are coupons. FOLLOWS: true in all 8 possible pictures (checked by computer).
3. Idea in one line: Chain the two 'All' statements: coupons sit inside offers, offers sit inside discounts. Reversing an 'All' is never allowed, but because coupons exist and all of them are discounts, at least one discount is a coupon.
