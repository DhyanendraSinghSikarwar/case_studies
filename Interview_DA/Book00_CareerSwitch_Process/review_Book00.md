## Known limits (honest status)
- Book 0 has no executable content; its quality depends entirely on the research files, which are non-random (JD sample n=186, about +/-7 points; sub-samples wider) and mostly search-summary level for interviews. Glassdoor, AmbitionBox, Reddit, Quora and Naukri content was not accessible.
- Pay: only about 31 of 186 JDs show pay (0 of 25 for the GCC sample); blog, vendor and snippet figures are unverified by design; the Rs 6-10 LPA band rests on thin evidence and the guide says so.
- No data exists in the research on: switch success rates, outcomes by age, bootcamp placement, certificate value in India, the size of the HR-to-analyst pay change, probation/bond/background-check norms. These are answered "No reliable data found" plus a decision rule.
- Source conflicts are stated rather than resolved: Deutsche Bank Workday role "Entry" (MS Section 6/Appendix A) vs "Workday-reporting roles asked 3+ years" (MS Addendum); Meesho/PhonePe round counts; IP tallies ("about 19 of 20" vs about 17 after exclusions; guesstimate tally counts Fractal twice).
- General-knowledge statements (what a GCC is, Workday/Prism description, platform names) are labelled "general knowledge, not checked" where they appear.
- Book/Part pointers follow plan.md Section 6; Books 3-12 are not written yet, so pointers describe nothing beyond the plan.
- A read-through by a non-technical reader is still advisable.
