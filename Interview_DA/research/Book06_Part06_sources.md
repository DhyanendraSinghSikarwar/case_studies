## Star reasoning
Stars are judgement; sources are thin and mostly prep sites. Three-star: core P&C and basic coin/dice/card/ball probability, which every source lists for TCS, Infosys, Wipro and Cognizant style tests and which are quick marks. Two-star: restricted arrangements, conditional and independent-event items, leap-year and rank patterns (frequent in prep banks, listed 0-2 per test). One-star: derangements, three-dice sums, stars and bars, digit-sum shortcuts, circular alternation; differentiators for Fractal or Mu Sigma style harder gates, not evidenced as frequent.
