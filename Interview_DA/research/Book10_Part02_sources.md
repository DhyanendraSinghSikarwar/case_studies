## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives question frequencies. 3 stars: vocabulary and data-model basics every e-commerce analyst round assumes (business model, order lifecycle, cancellation/return/RTO, SKU, dark store, 10-minute promise, COD, tables, delivery-time terms), plus the core metric calculations that the Flipkart/Swiggy guides and KPI pages repeat (GMV-to-net-sales waterfall, on-time delivery, return rate, fill rate, availability, payment success, new vs repeat, dashboard and MIS builds) and three diagnostic cases (returns, late delivery, COD RTO) because Flipkart reviews report KPI and case rounds. 2 stars: seller/ads/search/pricing/support/rider/workforce metrics, privacy awareness and operational cases; reasonable for good candidates but thinly evidenced. 1 star: regulation detail, buy box, settlement, scorecard design and causal-analysis cases; differentiators for product companies.
