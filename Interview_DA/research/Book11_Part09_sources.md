## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives frequencies. 3 stars: blanking, nerves, unknown question or tool, clarifying, mistakes under observation, connection drops; these are what every guide covers and what career switchers fear most. 2 stars: difficult or sceptical interviewers, silence, interruptions, panels, virtual setup, inappropriate questions. 1 star: edge cases (no-AI rule, interviewer factual error, follow-up email, recovery after a bad interview).
