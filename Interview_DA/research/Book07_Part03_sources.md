## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
Evidence is practice sites plus a handful of candidate reports (Swiggy orders per day, Delhi Metro riders, Flipkart sale orders, Uber airport revenue, Zomato fleet), so no question claims a verified company origin. 3 stars: the classic "orders per day", "metro riders", "rides per day", rider-fleet and order-to-revenue conversion prompts, plus simple conversions that any such round needs; 2 stars: capacity, roster, call-centre, truck and Little's-law style operations calculations, revenue-per-venue estimates, and defence-of-assumption questions; 1 star: niche or differentiator prompts (EV charging, warehouse area, analytics-team payroll). 24 of 72 entries are fully specified numeric prompts (plus about 15 open estimation prompts that are self-contained) with inline inputs and a single checkable figure; most of the rest are open estimation prompts or short concept questions. All inline figures are inputs invented for the question, not real statistics; answer agents must label base numbers "assumed/approximate, unverified". Gap-fills (not mapped to a found source): about 40 entries (roster, call-centre, hub, SaaS, churn and cost-per-cancellation calculations, concept questions on peak factors and sensitivity).
