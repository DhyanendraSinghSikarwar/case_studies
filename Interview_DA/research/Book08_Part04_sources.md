## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives frequencies. Every question bank found leads with "metric dropped, how would you investigate", so 3 stars go to the core frame (clarify, validate data, segment, funnel, price-volume, mix shift, noise vs signal, communication) and HR equivalents (attrition, time-to-hire, offer acceptance); 2 stars to second-line traps (lag, duplicates, time zones, denominators, outliers, tracking changes, small-sample NPS, causal separation); 1 star to differentiators (Goodhart, difference-in-differences, anomaly-detection design, migration reconciliation, post-mortems). Difficulty follows the arithmetic and reasoning load.
