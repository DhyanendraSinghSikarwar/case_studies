- Three stars: chart selection, fastest chart creation, dashboard definition and elements, interactive dashboard build, slicer-to-many-pivots, combo/secondary axis. - Two stars: format/definition questions repeated across the lists (clustered vs stacked, pie, data series, sparklines, trendline, waterfall, histogram, Pareto) plus practical dashboard tasks (KPI cards, dynamic charts, refresh, protection).
- One star: niche or differentiator charts (box and whisker, map, gauge, Gantt, form controls) and design-critique/performance questions.
