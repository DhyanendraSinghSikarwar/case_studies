## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives frequencies. 3 stars: the questions that appear in nearly every guide and bank (STAR itself, teamwork, learning quickly, strength/weakness, mistake, deadline, conflict, failure, prioritising, feedback, using data, plus the switcher-specific "no analytics experience" prompts). 2 stars: common but less universal stories (initiative, change, ownership, ethics, confidentiality, error in a report, bad news, pushing back) and STAR-technique probes. 1 star: differentiators, harder influence/setback prompts and low-frequency prompts.
