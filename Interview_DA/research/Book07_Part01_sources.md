## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
Evidence: guesstimates are reported for Mu Sigma, Fractal, Flipkart, Meesho and Indium (about 6 profiles in the internal research; strong theme across prep sites) but all are anecdotal or prep-site claims, so no "asked at" claim is made per question. Stars are judgement: 3 stars for method, assumptions, clarifying questions, top-down vs bottom-up, sanity checks, household/segmentation of population and basic calculation drills, as every guesstimate round uses them; 2 stars for driver trees, peaks/averages, ranges, error-compounding and handling base numbers you do not know; 1 star for differentiators (calibration anchors, triangulation, unit analysis, debugging an off-by-10x answer, practice plan). The target of 30 was exceeded (32) to include the range, error-compounding and debugging question types. Roughly 40% are numeric/specified prompts. Unverified: exact frequency of guesstimates at Hyderabad GCC/service-firm interviews (mostly Bengaluru analytics firms and startups in the evidence); gap-fill questions (not directly supported by a named source but standard framework topics): the range-presentation, error-compounding, calibration-anchor, unit-analysis and practice-plan questions (5).
