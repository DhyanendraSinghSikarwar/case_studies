## 1. Method and limitations (read first)

**How evidence was gathered.** About 24 web searches (WebSearch) plus 16 direct page fetches (WebFetch) on the companies named in the brief. The searches returned Glassdoor, AmbitionBox, GeeksforGeeks (GfG), LeetCode, Naukri Code360, InterviewQuery, Dataford, Hyring, FACE Prep and PlacementPreparation pages.

**Access problems (recorded as required by style_guide Section 8).**

| Site | What happened | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Glassdoor (all country domains) | WebFetch returned HTTP 403 | Glassdoor content below comes only from the search tool's text summaries of those pages. I could not open the original review. Treated as secondary. |
| AmbitionBox | HTTP 403 | Same. AmbitionBox appeared in results but its content could not be read. No AmbitionBox claims are made. |
| LeetCode Discuss | HTTP 403 | Amazon BA-II post known only via summary. |
| Naukri Code360 | HTTP 403 | Swiggy post not read. |
| Reddit (r/developersIndia etc.) | WebSearch rejected `reddit.com`; WebFetch refused it | **No Reddit evidence at all.** |
| LinkedIn posts, YouTube transcripts | Not surfaced by any search; not accessed | **No LinkedIn or YouTube evidence.** |
| InterviewQuery, Dataford (second fetch) | HTTP 429 (rate limited) | Used only via search summaries. |

**Pages actually read by me (primary reads):** GfG Tiger Analytics data analyst campus post, GfG Tiger Senior Analyst post, GfG Accenture DA post, GfG Amazon BIE post, GfG Deloitte USI 2021, GfG Ugam 2021, GfG LatentView 2020, FACE Prep Mu Sigma guide, Hyring PhonePe guide. Everything else is "via search summary", which means the search tool paraphrased the page and I could not verify the wording. Treat those as lower-confidence.

**Other limitations.**
- Reports are anonymous, self-reported, and span 2009 to 2026. I flag date wherever known. Where date is unknown it is marked "date unknown".
- Many reports do not state role, city or seniority. Several are campus-drive reports, which differ from lateral/switcher hiring (the book's target reader).
- Aggregator guides (InterviewQuery, Dataford, Hyring, FACE Prep) are third-party syntheses. Their stated round counts conflict with each other and with candidate reports. They are cited as "guide", never as evidence of company policy.
- No company publishes an official DA interview structure in anything I found. All "process" statements are candidate-reported. Nothing here is verified by the companies.
- Counts of "sources supporting" a pattern count distinct pages/reports that I saw or that the summary attributed; they are small (usually 1 to 4). A pattern with fewer than 3 independent sources is labelled "anecdote".
- Hyderabad and Bengaluru specificity is weak. Many reports are for Chennai, Pune, Noida, Mumbai or unspecified locations. Location is stated per item.

**Confidence labels used:** [R] = I read the page; [S] = search-tool summary only; [G] = third-party guide; unverified = single weak source or no source.

## 2. Per-company-type findings

### 2.1 Service MNCs (TCS, Infosys, Accenture, Deloitte USI, Cognizant, Wipro)

**Number of rounds.** Reports range 2 to 4. Typical shape: online aptitude/assessment, technical interview, (sometimes managerial/project), HR.
- TCS: a guide says 3 rounds (technical, managerial, HR), resume-driven [G] https://www.interviewquery.com/guides/tata-consultancy-services-data-analyst . One Hyderabad Glassdoor review mentions an automated "bot-run" interview and no follow-up [S] https://static.glassdoor.com.mx/Interview/Tata-Consultancy-Services-Interview-E13461-RVW94956803.htm (role was data engineer, not DA). Post-NQT interview reported covering projects, OOPS, DSA, SQL and HR [S] https://placementpreparation.io/blog/?p=6710 .
- Infosys: Glassdoor reviewers describe online assessment (aptitude + basic analytics), technical (SQL, Excel, Python, Power BI/Tableau, data interpretation, case), HR [S] https://www.glassdoor.co.uk/Interview/Infosys-Interview-E7927-RVW98397795.htm ; guide says 2-3 rounds [G] https://www.interviewquery.com/guides/infosys-data-analyst . One Hyderabad review (interview Mar 2026) reports 4 rounds: aptitude, a technical test on LLMs/RAG/GenAI, a technical interview on past GenAI projects and situational decisions, HR [S] https://www.glassdoor.ca/Interview/Infosys-Interview-E7927-RVW103925072.htm . Role was not confirmed as DA in the summary. **Single anecdote**.
- Accenture India: aptitude/cognitive + technical + communication assessment + interview(s). One Bengaluru report: online elimination round (verbal, logical, quant, core CS incl. databases, 1-2 coding problems) then a non-elimination English communication assessment [G/S] https://interviewquery.com/interview-guides/accentureindia-data-analyst . A Jan 2026 Bangalore-Rural report: aptitude test then technical on Python, SQL, basic statistics; INNER vs LEFT JOIN asked [S] https://www.glassdoor.sg/Interview/Accenture-Interview-E4138-RVW95521630.htm . A GfG DA post lists phone screen, technical (SQL, Excel, data scenarios), behavioural, case/technical, final, with no questions given, no location, no interview date [R] https://geeksforgeeks.org/my-data-analyst-interview-experience-at-accenture . Dataford says its aggregate of 579 reports rates the process medium difficulty with about a 58% offer rate [G, unverified method] https://dataford.io/interview-guides/accenture-in-india .
- Deloitte USI (Hyderabad): reviewers in 2025-26 report 2 rounds. (a) Sept 2025: round 1 SQL basics, window functions, duplicate detection/removal, query optimisation; round 2 Power BI (row-level security, optimisation, SUM vs SUMX) and project workflow; about a week wait [S] https://www.glassdoor.com.au/Interview/Deloitte-Interview-E2763-RVW89128406.htm (exact review ID mapping not verified; the summary cited Hyderabad reviews listed at https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Deloitte-Interview-E2763-RVW95203922.htm and https://static.glassdoor.com.mx/Interview/Deloitte-Interview-E2763-RVW102419238.htm ). (b) Analyst trainee 2026: introduction, SQL query merging five tables, Python questions. (c) Analyst Nov 2025: interactive aptitude-based first round, second round project explanation plus two medium-hard SQL questions, candidate solved one. 2021 campus (GfG): AMCAT 75-min aptitude (about 300 sat, 103 shortlisted), then one technical-cum-HR round, easy; questions were ML/AI basics, a diamond pattern print, resume projects, "5 years" HR question; 36 of 103 selected [R] https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/?p=663676 .
- Cognizant (Hyderabad): July 2025 report: 2 technical rounds (SQL, business analysis, stakeholder management) + casual HR [S] https://fr.glassdoor.ca/Entretien/Cognizant-Entretien-E8014-RVW99069017.htm ; Feb 2025 report (role unstated): online communication round, MCQ+coding with SELECT/JOIN/alias SQL, in-person round focused on Java with two SQL questions, process 3 months [S] https://static.glassdoor.com.ar/Interview/Cognizant-Interview-E8014-RVW95819294.htm . Power BI only appears in an older 2022 report (slicers, filters, modelling, RLS), no city [S].
- Wipro: guide says 3 rounds: online assessment (aptitude: logical, quant, verbal; sometimes coding), technical (SQL, analysis, Excel/Power BI, projects), HR [G] https://interviewquery.com/interview-guides/wipro-data-analyst . A 2023 Bangalore-Rural candidate reported Power BI, DAX (SUM, SUMX, DATEDIFF, AVG), SQL basics, joins, CTE, stored procedures; a 2025 Noida candidate got joins, GROUP BY/HAVING, department-highest-salary query [S] https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Interview/Wipro-Interview-E9936-RVW74123372.htm and https://static.glassdoor.com.mx/Interview/Wipro-Interview-E9936-RVW103108719.htm (mapping of which review holds which detail not verified). One reviewer saw a technical round on ML and statistics (anecdote).

**Question types (service MNCs).** SQL (joins, GROUP BY/HAVING, second/Nth highest salary, window functions, CTE, duplicates, subqueries), Power BI/DAX concepts (RLS, SUM vs SUMX, ALL vs ALLEXCEPT, incremental refresh), basic Python/pandas, basic stats, project walk-through, aptitude, HR (career goals, relocation).
**Difficulty.** Mostly basic-to-medium. Deloitte campus 2021 "easy" [R]; Deloitte Nov 2025 candidate solved one of two medium-hard SQL [S]. Accenture rated medium by Dataford aggregate [G].
**What interviewers look for (as stated by sources).** Resume/project depth ("every point on the resume was probed" - Deloitte 2015 Hyderabad BI review [S]; TCS guide "resume-driven" [G]; Wipro "walk through a personal project" and communication quality [G]); Accenture explicit English communication check [S/G].
**Timelines.** Deloitte: about a week wait after round 2 [S]. TCS guide: 1-2 weeks [G]. Cognizant: one candidate 3 months [S]. Accenture/Wipro: not reliably stated. Unverified in general.
**GenAI evidence.** One Infosys Hyderabad review (Mar 2026) only. See Section 5.
**GD.** None of the service-MNC sources above mention GD.

### 2.2 GCCs (JPMorgan, Wells Fargo, HSBC, Novartis, Amazon, Microsoft)

- **JPMorgan (Hyderabad).** Evidence is thin. One Hyderabad review (no offer) calls the process long with too many rounds and recalls basic Python questions [S] https://static.glassdoor.it/Interview/J-P-Morgan-Interview-E145-RVW90223368.htm (mapping unverified). A Dataford guide (not Hyderabad-specific) lists online assessment, recruiter screen, technical, superday, live coding; 4-6 weeks [G] https://dataford.io/interview-guides/jp-morgan/data-analyst . New York DA reviews list Excel, Python, SQL, statistics, Alteryx, dashboards [S] (not India; relevance low). Hyderabad intern listings mention 2-round and 4-round processes [S] https://myinternships.in/internships/jpmorgan-chase-sql-data-analyst-intern-hyderabad-4494da (job-board listings, weak). **Treat all of JPMorgan as unverified for full-time Hyderabad DA.**
- **Wells Fargo (Hyderabad).** Reviews are for Senior Analytics Consultant, not plain DA. Nov 2024: first round on Teams covering Tableau, SQL, logical reasoning; Tableau topics (blending, LODs, filters); SQL joins, TRUNCATE vs DELETE, views vs temp tables; another reviewer about an hour of basic-to-scenario SQL plus SSIS/SSRS, window functions, string functions; the in-person managerial round was almost non-technical except RANK vs DENSE_RANK; 3 rounds (technical, managerial, HR) in about four weeks; one candidate rejected as "not technically strong" [S] https://www.glassdoor.fr/Entretien/Wells-Fargo-Entretien-E8876-RVW93240107.htm and https://www.glassdoor.fr/Entretien/Wells-Fargo-Entretien-E8876-RVW92430216.htm . About 3-4 independent reviews.
- **HSBC (Hyderabad).** One Hyderabad Mar 2026 report: 2 rounds, technical (SQL, Python, banking functions, resume) then HR [S] https://www.glassdoor.fr/Entretien/HSBC-Entretien-E3482-RVW87819225.htm (mapping unverified). A Mumbai Jun 2026 report: 3 interview rounds + 2 HR, psychometric, Python, Power BI, Looker, pandas/matplotlib [S]. Glassdoor DA difficulty 2/5 with 50% positive on a small base [S]. **Anecdotes.**
- **Novartis (Hyderabad).** Varies widely. March 2023 report: 6 rounds (online technical, puzzles/SQL/Excel round, Excel case study with pivots, rank and formulas, technical round built on the case, senior management, HR) over 2 weeks; Sept 2025 campus report: online MCQ (maths, reasoning, puzzles), 4 technical rounds, HR, with a pie-chart interpretation question; another Sept 2025 report: group discussion, project interview, HR; one report mentions clinical trials/drug pipeline and basic Excel [S] https://www.glassdoor.co.uk/Interview/Novartis-Interview-E6667-RVW87067100.htm , https://www.glassdoor.sg/Interview/Novartis-Interview-E6667-RVW99893619.htm , https://www.glassdoor.ie/Interview/Novartis-Interview-E6667-RVW99908923.htm (which review holds which detail not verified). Roles are not confirmed as DA. **Anecdotes; strong signals for Excel case and a GD in one process.**
- **Amazon (Bengaluru/Hyderabad; BA, BIE).** Most consistent GCC pattern. LeetCode BA-II India post: 5 rounds, SQL screen with shared screen, then scenario/leadership-principle (LP) rounds, rounds held on Chime [S] https://leetcode.com/discuss/interview-experience/4118019/Business-Analyst-II-At-Amazon-India/ . GfG BIE Bangalore (fresh graduate, early 2023): 5 rounds: online analytical/data-interpretation test, technical SQL + data modelling, LP behavioural, data analysis/case study (customer behaviour, sales, supply chain), final with senior manager; no specific questions given [R] https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/?p=1046373 . Hyderabad Glassdoor report: rated Easy, SQL basic-medium (GROUP BY, joins, window functions) and basic Python (pandas, numpy) [S]. Bengaluru 2025: third-highest salary per department using DENSE_RANK; rejected after over a month with no explanation [S]. Seattle/Austin reports (US; not India) say mostly STAR behavioural [S]. About 4 India sources agree on: SQL early, LP-based behavioural in most later rounds.
- **Microsoft (Hyderabad/Bengaluru).** Very weak. Hyderabad DA: moderate, basic CTE SQL, calm interviewers; another Hyderabad review (Jan 2024) lists Power BI, Fabric, Azure, DSA, Excel; Bengaluru DA: HR screen, SQL technical, final case study with analytics team and hiring manager; one Bengaluru report "very hard" with coding/DSA [S]. InterviewQuery BA guide: hiring-manager call then loop of about four interviews, mostly behavioural (global, not India-specific) [G]. **Unverified for IDC.**

**GCC common pattern.** SQL (joins, window functions, ranking) is the one constant. Tools differ by team (Tableau at Wells Fargo, Power BI at Deloitte/Microsoft, Excel case at Novartis). Behavioural/LP weight is high at Amazon and probably Microsoft (guide-level evidence).
**Difficulty.** Basic-to-medium in India reports (Amazon Hyderabad "Easy", Microsoft Hyderabad moderate, HSBC 2/5). Not uniform.
**Timelines.** Wells Fargo about 4 weeks [S]; Novartis 2 weeks [S]; Amazon one candidate waited over a month for a rejection [S]; JPMorgan guide 4-6 weeks [G]. Otherwise unverified.
**GD.** Novartis (one Sept 2025 process) [S]. Otherwise none seen.
**GenAI.** None seen at GCCs.

SQL window-function scenarios, Python, Tableau/Power BI; some ML depth [S]. Guide: 4 rounds (online assessment, panel, manager/client, technical) [G] https://www.interviewquery.com/guides/latentview-analytics-data-analyst . - **Indium Software (Hyderabad/Chennai).** Most Hyderabad-relevant of this group. **Timelines.** Tiger one candidate 3 weeks [S]; Fractal and Mu Sigma not reliably stated. Mostly unverified.
**GD.** Appears at LatentView (2020 campus, [R]), Mu Sigma (group case activity per guide [R/G], GD in some candidate reports [S]), Indium (May 2026 [S]), Ugam (see 2.5). Not seen at Tiger or Fractal. 3-4 sources, **mostly campus and mostly pre-2023**, except Indium 2026.

## 5. Implications for plan.md Sections 3 and 6

1. **SQL (Book 2, 600 Qs): correct weighting; maybe push harder.** Strongest signal across all types. Startups and Amazon-style screens reach medium-hard (windows, CTE, recursive CTE, self-joins, cumulative sum, route-cost). Ensure Book 2 parts include recursive CTE, cumulative/running totals, de-duplication, Nth highest with DENSE_RANK, gaps/islands-type scenarios, and query optimisation. Service-MNC reports need only basic-medium, so tiering by company type (star ratings) fits plan.md Section 4.
2. **Stats/probability (Book 5): do not downsize.** Tiger (hardest part of interview), Fractal and Flipkart show Bayes, conditional probability, hypothesis testing, geometric probability. Probably under-represented if Book 5 is only "stats for analysts". Add a "probability puzzles and Bayes" part. Confidence: moderate (3-4 sources).
3. **Guesstimates/cases (Book 7) and Scenario (Book 8): well supported.** Mu Sigma, Fractal, Flipkart, Meesho, Indium, Amazon. Required content: structured assumptions, "metric dropped 15%" diagnosis, market entry/profitability. Add "KPI definition round" (Flipkart) and "dashboard metric decline" (Meesho). Moderate confidence.
4. **Aptitude (Book 6): keep.** Online assessments are the first gate at analytics firms (Fractal 75 Q incl. quant/logical/verbal and a video intro; Tiger 20 maths + 10 CS + 2 coding; Mu Sigma psychometric/video-observation), Accenture (cognitive plus communication), Deloitte (AMCAT). Add data interpretation (pie-chart questions at Novartis) and a short English/communication-test section for Accenture-type assessments; add a note that some assessments embed SQL/MCQ and basic CS (databases) questions.
5. **Power BI (Book 4): keep high priority, add DAX specifics.** Reported DAX/Power BI topics: SUM vs SUMX, ALL vs ALLEXCEPT, DATEDIFF, row-level security, incremental refresh, query optimisation, Desktop vs Service, gateways, data modelling, Power Query. Tableau appears in 4 profiles (Wells Fargo, Indium, LatentView, Infosys): the plan's "Tableau basics" depth is appropriate; do not enlarge.
6. **Excel (Book 1): possibly slightly over-weighted for interviews compared with SQL, but Novartis shows an Excel case study (pivots, rank, formulas) and Meesho/Accenture test Excel.** Evidence of Excel as a main interview topic is only about 5 profiles. Keep 600 but flag for Phase 0 JD data (market_skills_2026.md) before deciding. Not enough here to recommend cutting.
7. **Python (Book 3): weighted OK; reports ask basic-level tasks** (duplicate frequency, strings/lists, pandas date parsing, basic imputation) and Tiger asks LeetCode-easy style; no evidence of advanced Python for DA. Consider dedicating a part to "LeetCode-easy list/string/dict problems in Python" since Tiger, Accenture and Deloitte-style assessments include small coding. Moderate confidence.
8. **GenAI (Book 9): one report only.** Keep the AI-for-analysts section but small; focus on definitions (LLM, RAG, embeddings, prompt design, evaluating outputs, data privacy) plus "how I use AI tools in analysis"; do not expand beyond the plan until more evidence. Mark as "emerging".
9. **GD (Book 11): keep but short.** Evidence is concentrated on campus/early-career at analytics firms and one 2026 Indium (Hyderabad) report. Keep a compact GD playbook (about 20-30 Qs plus topics such as business and current affairs), not a major part.
10. **Book 0 (process guide) Section 3: restructure by evidence.** 
    - Service MNCs: online assessment, technical (SQL/Power BI/Excel/projects), HR. Add Accenture communication assessment.
    - GCCs: SQL screen; tool-specific round; managerial; Amazon LP behavioural. State that Wells Fargo/HSBC/JPMorgan evidence is thin.
    - Analytics firms: aptitude gate, 1-2 technical (SQL/Python/probability), case/guesstimate, client round at Indium, HR.
    - Startups: SQL online test, hard SQL round, case/metrics, manager/HR.
    - Add a "how reliable is this" note and recommend candidates verify with the recruiter.
11. **Behavioural/HR and project walk-through (Book 11):** ranks near SQL in frequency (project depth about 14 profiles). Add a dedicated "explain your project end to end" section and Amazon Leadership Principles STAR bank (Amazon is the only company with consistent LP evidence). This is likely under-weighted if Book 11 is mostly HR-switch storytelling. Include "career switch from HR" prompts as planned.
12. **Missing/under-represented in plan:** (a) data modelling basics (star schema, normalisation) appear at Flipkart, Amazon BIE, Wipro (stored procedures): ensure coverage in Book 2 or 4. (b) SSIS/SSRS/stored procedures at Wells Fargo/Wipro (legacy BI stack): optional "good-to-have", do not add a book. (c) Cloud/Azure/Fabric: 1 Microsoft report; keep as good-to-have. (d) Basic CS/DSA/OOP questions occur in Accenture OA, Tiger OA, Deloitte (pattern printing), TCS/Cognizant (Java/OOP): suggest a small "basic programming and CS fundamentals for DA assessments" part in Book 3 or 6.
13. **Plan Section 2 table corrections to suggest.** "Hyderabad/Bengaluru reviews mention guesstimates, scenario questions, window functions, CTEs": supported for window functions/CTEs (many), guesstimates (Flipkart, Mu Sigma, Fractal, Meesho; Bengaluru/Chennai-based mostly) but Hyderabad specifically is only supported for Deloitte, Indium, Cognizant, Wells Fargo, HSBC, Novartis, Amazon (SQL/tool-focused). The typical loop "online test (aptitude + SQL) -> technical -> HR" is supported at service MNCs and analytics firms; for GCCs and startups the SQL screen replaces the aptitude test.
