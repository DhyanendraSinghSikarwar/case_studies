## Star-assignment reasoning
No source gives frequency for these questions. Stars are judgement: three stars for questions every switcher must settle before applying (format, bullets, summary, keywords, the "no analytics experience" interview question, which datasets are safe, project walk-through, README, safe publishing); two stars for common follow-ups; one star for edge cases and AI/ATS-tool questions. Project walk-through frequency is the only sourced anchor (about 14 of 20 interview profiles, IP Section 4, candidate-reported).

## Limits
Search summaries only; blog advice is vendor-written and unmeasured; US-centric on page length and gaps; no India-specific recruiter-behaviour data. The outline therefore uses decision rules, not statistics.
