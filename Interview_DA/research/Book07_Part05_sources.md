## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
Evidence that a "metric/sales dropped X%" case is asked is consistent across the internal interview research (about 8 profiles name a case or metric diagnosis) and every question bank seen; but company-verified reports are thin. Stars are therefore judgement: 3 stars for the core method (clarify, validate data, price/volume/mix, funnel, segment, hypothesis tree, internal vs external) and the simple numeric decompositions and the Tuesday case, because every reported variant uses them; 2 stars for numeric cases with mix shift, cohorts, DiD, marketplace-side and monitoring questions, which appear in stronger rounds; 1 star for differentiators (Pareto, fishbone, synthetic control, interrupted time series, timezone and bot artefacts, auto-RCA design). About 55% of entries have inline data or a derivable figure. Gap-fills (not directly seen in sources): about 30 entries on data artefacts, stock-out censoring, calendar effects (4 vs 5 Tuesdays, per-day normalisation), percentage vs percentage-point, payroll and attendance flavour, ride-hailing, lending and SaaS MRR bridge. The "orders fell on Tuesdays" wording is the brief's own example; no source found it verbatim. Unverified: which of the question-bank prompts were really asked at the tagged company.
