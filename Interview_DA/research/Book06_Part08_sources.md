Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured frequency)
- 3 stars: percent change, shares from pie/table, averages, ratios, profit percent, simple table and bar lookups, line-chart growth, percentage points versus percent, read-the-chart basics. Every source lists these as the core of any DI set.
- 2 stars: weighted averages, two-pie combinations, funnels, index numbers, missing-value pies, pie plus table mixes, trap questions about shares versus absolutes, misleading axes.
- 1 star: frequency tables and mid-point means, moving averages, Pareto, waterfall, dual axes, chart-choice concepts, caselets and timing strategy; differentiators or rare in campus gates.

Limitations
- All evidence is prep-site, vendor and search-snippet level; no source gives true frequency, and company attributions (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, Accenture) come from prep sites and are unverified. Sample: about 7 searches, roughly 100 result titles, no page read in full.
- Questions use invented but internally consistent data sets (business and HR themes) written for this book; no question was copied from a source.
- About 35 of 94 questions are concept or method items and gap-fills (chart choice, histogram vs bar, dual axis, scatter causality, Pareto, waterfall, moving average, caselet and time-budget questions) added from standard practice rather than seen in a named source.
