Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured frequency; sources are prep sites)
- 3 stars: basic syllogism conclusions, contrapositive/equivalence, "only" statements and rule-violation checks. Syllogisms and statement-conclusion items recur in every company page found (TCS NQT, Infosys, Wipro, Mu Sigma); conditional rules underlie data-validation questions.
- 2 stars: three- and four-statement chains, either-or, negation, Wason-style rule checking, set-bound items, common named fallacies. Plausible in gates but each less certain.
- 1 star: possibility classification across four conclusions, set lower-bound puzzles, less common fallacy names. Differentiators at analytics firms; no source gives frequency.

Limitations
- 4 searches, all commercial prep or encyclopedia sites; no official test-maker material. Counts and timings conflict between sources and are not used as facts in the questions.
- All 73 questions are original constructions on topics the sources name; none was seen verbatim, so all are gap-fills at the question level. Only the topic coverage and star levels trace to sources.
- Syllogism items use the convention "some = at least one; every named group is non-empty", stated in each question and in the Scope line. 20 syllogism, either-or and classification items (18 syllogisms plus 2 classification items; some of the 18 are either-or or none-follow items) and the 2 selection puzzles (5 panels, 2 task selections) were brute-force checked by code (Venn-region enumeration or combination enumeration) for a unique answer; the remaining conditional, table and set-bound items were checked by hand.
