## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives frequencies. Stars are judgement: 3 stars for the mismatch-reconciliation family and "dashboard is slow/stale" diagnoses, because every question bank found leads with them (Dataford, Looker-vs-BigQuery posts, Power BI and Tableau question banks) and Deloitte USI reports mention Power BI optimisation; 2 stars for design, refresh-architecture, trust and governance scenarios and for definitional traps (as-of headcount, partial-month comparisons, fiscal year); 1 star for differentiators (currency, SCD history, approximate distinct counts, retirement of dashboards, removing a red KPI). Difficulty is judgement too.

- All figures in questions are invented for exercises, not real statistics. 22 of 70 questions (31%) carry inline numbers with a checkable figure; the rest are structured-diagnosis or communication questions.
