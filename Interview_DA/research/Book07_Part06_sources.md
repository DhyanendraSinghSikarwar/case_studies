## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
Evidence says profitability and growth cases appear at Mu Sigma, Fractal, Flipkart, Meesho and Swiggy (anecdote and prep-site claims). No source ranks sub-types by frequency. So stars are judgement: 3 stars for the profit tree, contribution margin, break-even, unit economics, price-increase decisions and the core numeric items any such round would use; 2 stars for specific pricing methods, channel and cohort economics, bundling, market entry and diagnostic multi-step cases; 1 star for secondary or differentiating questions (operating leverage, psychological pricing, student discount, markdown, growth versus profit). About 60% of questions carry inline numbers so a single figure can be checked. Gap-fills (not tied to a found source): operating leverage, product-line shutdown, price-volume bridge, hotel RevPAR, BPO and attrition economics, referral and bundle pricing, and the clarifying-questions / 60-second recommendation items, about 12 in all. Unverified: that named Indian companies ask these exact cases; the Swiggy, Flipkart and Tiger Analytics links are only snippets.
