## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives frequencies. 3 stars: principles whose sample questions recur across the Glassdoor and prep-site snippets (Dive Deep, Customer Obsession, Ownership, Bias for Action, Deliver Results, Have Backbone, Earn Trust), plus the STAR, probing and no-experience questions every career switcher needs. 2 stars: other principles asked in loops but without specific India evidence. 1 star: Think Big, Best Employer, Success and Scale, Hire and Develop (more relevant to senior roles) and HR-specific framing.
