## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
Evidence says metric definitions (DAU/MAU, retention, churn, CAC, LTV, conversion) are the most consistently named topic for product/business analyst rounds (several prep sources; Flipkart KPI round; Swiggy BA guides), but no source gives frequencies, so stars are judgement. 3 stars: core definitions, one-step calculations and the primary/guardrail metric framing plus the standard DAU-flat, conversion-rise and cohort-reading questions. 2 stars: adjacent metrics (GMV, MRR/NRR, NPS, abandonment, repeat rate), multi-step calculations, HR metrics (hiring funnel, cost per hire; supported by HR-analytics prep sites only) and KPI design for teams. 1 star: differentiators (K-factor, Rule of 40, discounted LTV, L28, take-rate trade-offs). Roughly 45% of entries carry inline numbers. HR/people flavour is about 8 entries. Gap-fills (not directly evidenced by the searches, added as important missing types): the mix-shift/Simpson's paradox retention question, order decomposition into user and frequency effects, cash vs LTV:CAC, lending LTV without full tenure, and the steady-state subscriber base. Unverified: that any listed question was asked verbatim at a named company.
