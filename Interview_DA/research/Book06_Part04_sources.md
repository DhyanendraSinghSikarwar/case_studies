Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured frequency)
- 3 stars: SI/CI formulas, find P/R/T, months-based SI, CI annual and half-yearly, doubling/tripling, CI-SI difference shortcut, partition of a sum. These are the 5-question-type core in every prep source and appear in company-tagged pages for TCS/Wipro/Cognizant/Infosys-type gates.
- 2 stars: growth/depreciation word problems, varying rates, finding rate/time from two amounts, CAGR, present value, Rule of 72, flat vs reducing EMI, ROI, break-even, payback, NPV, margins. Common in aptitude lists and plausible in analyst interviews; no source gave a frequency.
- 1 star: effective annual rate, nominal vs real rate, IRR, true discount/present worth, installment problems, 2/10 net 30. Occasional or differentiator.

Limitations
- Sample size: 6 successful searches, all prep-site snippets. No company publishes its question bank; per-assessment question counts (3-5 SI/CI per test) are a single aggregator's claim and unverified. Company attributions (TCS, Wipro, Cognizant, Infosys) are from tags on prep sites, not from verified candidate reports.
- All numbers in the questions were authored for this book; none is copied from a source. About 6 mirror a pattern seen in snippets (5,625 at 7.5%; doubles in 8 years, triple; effective annual rate of 20% quarterly; half-yearly compounding; headcount/CI at 10% for 2 years). About 17 are gap-fills from standard practice rather than seen in a source: ROI, break-even, payback, NPV, margins, depreciation, EMI (flat and reducing), IRR, nominal vs real, 2/10 net 30, CAGR, present value, Rule of 72 (the last two searches failed, so these rely on general knowledge).
- Installment questions state their convention in the text because conventions differ between textbooks; answer agents should honour the stated convention.
