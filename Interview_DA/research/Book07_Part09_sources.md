## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source ranks learning-journey questions by frequency. Stars are judgement based on (a) the internal research that structure and assumptions are what Mu Sigma, Fractal, Flipkart and Meesho-type rounds score, and (b) the fact that "how did you learn it / rate yourself / hardest part / show a real example" are generic openers for career switchers (same logic as Book 5 and Book 3 final parts). 3 stars: definition of structured thinking, MECE, first step on a vague problem, learning timeline, self-rating, real example, hardest topic, driver trees, thinking aloud. 2 stars: resources, order, weekly time, preparation plans, mocks, AI use, commercial awareness, answer-first communication. 1 star: certifications and casebooks, portfolio, 30/90-day plans, self-test design, teaching others, pushback handling, no-mentor practice, differentiators. About 40% of entries are plan-scope questions (resources, schedules, self-tests, portfolio), the rest learning-journey questions. Unverified: that interviewers ask these learning-journey questions in case rounds specifically; they are mainly HR and manager round material. Gap-fills (not in the instruction's example list, added for coverage): about 14, including MECE checking, clarifying questions, order-of-magnitude sanity checks, hypothesis prioritisation, recommendation from analysis, incomplete data, and when to stop structuring.
