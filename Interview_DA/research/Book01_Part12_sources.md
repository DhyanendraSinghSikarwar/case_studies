## C. Internal numbers used (not re-verified here)

- Pooled sample of 186 India analyst JDs (non-random, about plus or minus 7 points): Excel 112 (60%), SQL 54%, dashboards/reporting 75%, VBA/macros 13%, Google Sheets 8%, GenAI/AI tools 14%. Per-sample Excel: 60, 55, 48, 72, 76%.
- Certifications: none named as required in the samples; Tableau/Power BI, Google DA, MS Data Analyst Associate, CBAP only as advantages. No Excel certification (including MOS) was recorded in what the sub-agents tallied.
- interview_processes.md: about 5 profiles mention Excel (Novartis Excel case study, Accenture, Meesho MCQs, Infosys, JPMorgan NY).

## D. Star reasoning

- Stars 3 (Q1 to Q6): the learning-journey openers and the project walk-through are standard entry-level questions and follow the pattern of Book 2 Part 12; Excel appears in 60% of sampled JDs and MIS reports describe interviews built around Excel knowledge, so Q3 (version) and Q5 (a project, explaining every formula) are likely. This is judgement, not a measured frequency.
- Stars 2 (Q7 to Q18): practice, resources, datasets, test preparation (supported by MIS test reports), AI use (GenAI in 14% of JDs), Google Sheets (8%).
- Stars 1 (Q19 to Q24): certifications (none required in any sample), public portfolio, plan, coaching: differentiators.

## E. Limitations and honesty notes

- No source was found that reports "how did you learn Excel" style questions at a named company. All "learning journey" questions are standard-opener inferences, as in the Book 2 Part 12 list, not sourced reports. Of 24 questions, 6 touch source evidence (Q2, Q3, Q5, Q14, Q15, Q19); 18 are gap-fills.
- Not checked: any YouTube channel, Coursera, Udemy (a Udemy MO-200 course appeared in search results only), LinkedIn Learning, GeeksforGeeks, Kaggle general listings. Glassdoor, Naukri, Chandoo were blocked or returned no usable page.
- Search results include low-quality mirror and spam pages (noted by the search tool); none were used.
- Searches run (8): self-rating in Excel; India MIS/fresher Excel questions; MOS MO-200 cost; free Microsoft Excel training; free Excel/HR practice datasets; Excel version and XLOOKUP availability; Excel 2016/2019 end of support; MIS Excel test candidate experiences.
