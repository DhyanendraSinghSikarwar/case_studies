## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives frequencies. 3 stars: openers every guide implies (how did you learn, why ML as an analyst, which AI tools, walk through a project, hardest concept, no-degree background, understanding vs copying), because ML-touching rounds usually probe projects and learning story. 2 stars: resources, staying current, self-rating, learning order, project choice, explaining to managers, bad-output story, 30-day plan, self-tests. 1 star: certificate weight, 90-day plan, stop-learning-start-building, privacy rules, progress tracking, limits of knowledge, two-week model scenario, two-year outlook (differentiators).
