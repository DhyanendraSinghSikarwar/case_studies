## Star reasoning
- Dashboards/reporting is the most frequent non-SQL/Excel JD skill (52.5%), and "how do you design a dashboard / present to non-technical people" appears in several prep lists: general principles, chart choice, KPI selection, design approach, non-technical audience, number mismatch and low adoption got three stars.
- Specific design topics (colour, pie charts, hierarchy, accessibility, mobile, conditional formatting, HR dashboards) are standard follow-ups: two stars.
- Theory-named items (Tufte, Gestalt, preattentive attributes), niche visuals (waterfall, box plot), and stakeholder-conflict scenarios: one star (differentiators).
