# Book: *Logical Thinking from Zero, for Future Data Scientists*

**Scope:** Logic, reasoning, critical thinking, and problem solving. Math, Python, and statistics are excluded (learned separately).

**Standard structure for every chapter**

- Concept (plain language)
- 5 solved examples (step-by-step with the "why")
- 2 real-life scenarios (commerce, data, daily life)
- 15 practice questions (easy, medium, hard)
- Mistake log and 5-line recap

---
