## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
Evidence says metric-drop/funnel diagnosis and "how would you measure a feature" recur across many prep banks and appear in Flipkart, Meesho, Mu Sigma, Fractal and Tiger reports (per internal research), so 3 stars go to feature success metrics, guardrails, funnel arithmetic and segmentation, real-vs-artefact checks, and the core numeric cases (mix shift, selection bias, cannibalisation, A/B readout). 2 stars go to cohort retention, onboarding funnels (fintech, edtech, ride-hailing, marketplace), instrumentation, incrementality, rollout gating and HR-app flavour. 1 star goes to differentiators (marketplace interference, long-term holdouts, AI feature metrics, SRM, metric gaming). Part 7 is a difficulty-red topic; about 60% of questions carry inline numbers or tables so a figure can be checked. Gap-fills (not directly evidenced): roughly 15 (cannibalisation, difference-in-differences, crash-rate, membership, scheduling-sizing, HR leave/onboarding/payroll-bot items, SRM, build break-even). Unverified: that these exact prompts occur at named Indian firms.
