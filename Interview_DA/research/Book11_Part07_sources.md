## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives frequencies. 3 stars: what a GD is and is scored on, entering, opening, interruptions, concluding, how much to speak, and the AI-and-jobs topic (the most repeated 2025-26 topic across the lists). 2 stars: preparation, dominant/quiet members, disagreement, other sample topics, case-style GD, using numbers, deadlock. 1 star: virtual GD glitches, biased remarks, analytics-specific angles, nomination of peers (one report), one-chance speaking. Stars are deliberately modest because the round is rare for lateral hires.
