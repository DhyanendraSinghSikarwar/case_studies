## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives frequencies. Stars are judgement: 3 stars for the ambiguous-request, urgent-deadline, conflicting-definition, pushback, error-correction, scope-creep and caveat-communication scenarios, because every prep bank found leads with them and communication is a 90% must-have in the JD sample; 2 stars for variations (privacy-sensitive asks, causal claims, survey and denominator pitfalls, KPI redefinition, intake management); 1 star for differentiators (political requests, intake design, role-plays, layoff exclusion ethics). Difficulty 🟢🟡🔴 is also judgement. Only 8 of 79 questions carry inline data (each with one checkable figure), since stakeholder scenarios are mainly structured-answer questions.
