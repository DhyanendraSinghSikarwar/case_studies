## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives frequencies. Stars are judgement: 3 stars for the core questions every source set repeats (dimensions of quality, first checks, missing-value and duplicate handling, source mismatch and reconciliation, communicating an error, the pipeline-bug-after-reporting scenario) plus the basic derivations; 2 stars for specific wrong-data patterns (units, currency, dates, test data, joins fan-out, survivorship, late data) and HR-flavoured checks; 1 star for differentiators (golden record, schema drift, heaping, MNAR diagnosis, rehire linking). About 30% of questions (22) carry inline exercise data with a checkable figure.
