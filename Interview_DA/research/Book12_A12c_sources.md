## Star-assignment reasoning
Stars are judgement, not frequency data (no source gives question frequency). Rule used: three stars for decisions every switcher faces (do I need a certificate, what to track, weekly plan, notice period); two for common how-tos; one for edge cases. Colour: green for yes/no or simple how-to, yellow for judgement or multi-step, red for sensitive/high-stakes (notice period conflicts, scams).
