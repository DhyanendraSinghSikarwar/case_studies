## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives frequencies. Stars are judgement: 3 stars for core metric definitions and calculations (attrition, offer acceptance, cost per hire, funnel conversion, compa-ratio, pay gap) and the attrition/hiring-funnel/pay-gap diagnoses, because every HR-analytics question bank found centres on them; 2 stars for modelling, adverse impact variants, workforce planning, Simpson-type pitfalls and cost-of-attrition cases; 1 star for differentiators (internal mobility, calibration, L&D ROI, contract vs permanent, AI screening audit). About 55% of questions carry inline numbers or tables so a single figure can be checked.
