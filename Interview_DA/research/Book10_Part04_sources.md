## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives question frequencies. 3 stars: hospital vocabulary and the core KPIs (bed occupancy, ALOS, ARPOB, readmission), claims and denial basics, ICD-10, EMR data, patient-data handling, trial phases and primary/secondary sales, because every hospital, RCM and pharma source found names them. 2 stars: process vocabulary, more operational calculations (TAT, no-show, theatre utilisation, A/R days), pharma field-force metrics, privacy concepts (DPDP/HIPAA, anonymisation) and domain case questions. 1 star: specialist standards (SDTM/ADaM, HL7/FHIR, EDI), RWD, pharmacovigilance, market sizing and design tasks.
