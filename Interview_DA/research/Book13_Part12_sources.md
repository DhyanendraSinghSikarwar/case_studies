Limitations
- No source gives evidence that a specific reasoning study plan raises test scores or job outcomes; the answers must not claim effectiveness. The human-transfer evidence found was one snippet-level study plus opinion pieces.
- Roadmap shapes (phases, mocks late) come from prep blogs, some of which sell mock tests. Plan durations in answers are suggestions, not measured.
- All 23 questions are gap-fills (written from the style guide Section 7 requirements and the Book 9 Part 12 format); none was seen in a source. Two are small worked items: Q6 (week targets) and Q11 (seating enumeration, brute-force checked: unique answer B, C, A, D) plus Q12 (540 questions arithmetic, hand-checked: 36 days = 6 weeks, 45 min a day). 1 puzzle brute-force checked.
