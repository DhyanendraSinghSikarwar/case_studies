## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives frequencies for analyst interviews. 3 stars: "how do you use AI in your work", "how do you verify AI output", hallucination, privacy and what never to paste, prompt basics, AI-written SQL review and accountability, because every guide found stresses verification, risk and responsible use and these are the questions most likely if an interviewer touches AI. 2 stars: tool differences, context/iteration, AI for cleaning/summaries/learning, specific SQL/DAX/Excel checking tasks, enterprise vs consumer accounts, policy, prompt injection, DPDP. 1 star: differentiators and emerging topics (RAG definition, temperature, agents, evaluation set, semantic layer, licensing, design of safe workflows, text-to-SQL products).
