Star-assignment reasoning (judgement; no source gives a frequency per question)
- 2 stars: cognitive-reflex items, ages, Venn/set counting, pigeonhole, digit counts and bounding estimates. Sources list Venn/age/DI-style items in service-MNC tests and say analytics firms score structure and sanity checks; these are the most transferable skills for an analyst.
- 1 star: classic brain teasers (weighing, measuring, Josephus, tilings, cryptarithm, comparison counts). Candidate reports put them in analytics-firm interviews (Fractal, Mu Sigma, Tiger) with "limited pool" remarks, so occasional differentiators, not gates.
