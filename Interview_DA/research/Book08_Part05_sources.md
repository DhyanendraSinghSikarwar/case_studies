## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives question frequencies. 3 stars: universal frameworks and the commonest business analyses (churn, attrition, funnel, campaign, correlation vs causation, data checks, data-light time-pressure), because every guide and company pattern centres on structured approach and recommendation. 2 stars: specific analyses (seasonality, inventory, LTV, payroll anomalies, A/B inconclusive, ratio pitfalls). 1 star: differentiators (survival, retention-bonus selection, pay-gap defensibility, AI-screening bias, privacy, tooling limits).
