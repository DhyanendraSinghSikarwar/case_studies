## Limitations
- Accenture's communication assessment format is contradicted across sources (proprietary 6 sections/63 questions vs Versant spoken test vs written with an email task; elimination or not). The book therefore covers grammar, vocabulary, reading, jumbled sentences, email, conversation reply and spoken-repeat strategy, all labelled as format-unverified.
- Embedded CS items: DBMS presence at Accenture is disputed; Tiger is supported only by one institutional note plus snippets. The 11 or so SQL/CS items are standard fundamentals chosen as plausible, not as reported questions.
- Verbal ability searches for TCS/Infosys/Wipro failed (rate limit); RC, synonym and para-jumble items rest on general knowledge.

## Star-assignment reasoning
Stars are judgement because evidence is thin and conflicting. Three stars: grammar basics (agreement, tense, articles, prepositions), email writing and self-introduction (recur in every Accenture-format description), and core SQL/DBMS basics (WHERE vs HAVING, keys, NULL, joins) that appear in candidate reports and also in the SQL-heavy analyst gate. Two stars: vocabulary and voice/conditionals, normalisation, DELETE/TRUNCATE, ACID, pseudocode, spoken-repeat and workplace-reply items (reported in some formats). One star: odd-sentence, summary, confused words, networking, OOP and complexity items (occasional or single-source).
