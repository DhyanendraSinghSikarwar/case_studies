## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives frequencies. 3 stars: vocabulary and metrics that every BFSI guide and JD snippet repeats (NPA, GNPA/NNPA, DPD buckets, KYC, PD/LGD/EAD, credit score, collection efficiency, roll rate, loss/combined ratio, NIM, CASA, persistency, claim settlement ratio, fraud types, privacy of customer data). 2 stars: product and process knowledge, second-layer ratios, funnel and channel cuts, fintech payment metrics, insurance operations, regulation-awareness questions and the domain case studies, because they appear in single guides or JDs. 1 star: capital-markets basics, model-monitoring and advanced risk topics, and open design questions, as differentiators for 8-10 LPA or fintech roles.
