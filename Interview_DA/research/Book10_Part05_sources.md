## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives frequencies. 3 stars: core vocabulary and metrics every guide lists (OTIF, fill rate, turnover, lead time, safety stock, reorder point, stockout, forecast accuracy and bias, S&OP, ABC, data systems, supplier scorecard, three-way match). 2 stars: warehouse, logistics and procurement vocabulary, EOQ, service-level trade-off, XYZ, lead-time variability, master data, quick-commerce replenishment, and the investigation cases (late-delivery drop, fill rate vs OTIF, availability paradox). 1 star: differentiators for product or GCC roles (FVA, cash-to-cash, landed cost, Incoterms, e-way bill, VMI, network design, S&OP pack). Supply chain analyst roles are a minority of 0-3 year analyst postings, so no question is starred above what the general guides support.
