## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
No source gives frequencies. Stars are judgement from the recurring themes above: 3 stars for the reading routine, percent vs percentage-point, weighted averages, funnels, cohort tables, Simpson's-type reversals, median vs mean, misleading axes, correlation vs causation, small samples, HR metrics (attrition, hiring funnel, adverse impact, pay-gap by grade), reconciling mismatched totals; 2 stars for variance, mix/price-volume effects, seasonality, MAPE, concentration, distribution shape, definition changes, rating leniency; 1 star for differentiators (log scales, map choropleths, rebased indices, admitting a published error).
