Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured frequency)
- 3 stars: basic series (difference, square, alternating, Fibonacci-like), divisibility, unit digit, remainders, HCF/LCM word problems. These recur in every prep source and every service-MNC syllabus listing.
- 2 stars: wrong-term series, counting multiples, factors, trailing zeros, digit puzzles, AP/GP term questions, number-type definitions, Excel MOD (analyst flavour).
- 1 star: Fermat, last two digits, fractions LCM, binary conversion, grid puzzles, surds, 0.999... (differentiators or occasional).
Stars are judgement because sources are thin and give no frequencies.

Limitations
- Sample: about 7 searches, prep-site snippets only. Company attributions (Infosys 5-8 series items, TCS/Accenture syllabus) are unverified claims from prep sites and conflict with each other.
- Target was 50; 70 written to cover classic patterns, traps and AP/GP and Excel items.
- 27 of 70 questions are gap-fills from standard practice rather than seen in a source (sum of first n, payroll LCM, counting multiples, digit puzzle, AP/GP problems, Excel MOD, definitions, 0.999..., last two digits, etc.). Remaining 43 match topics/patterns named in the snippets above; exact wording is canonical, not copied.
- About 75% of the questions are fully specified numeric problems with a single checkable answer.
