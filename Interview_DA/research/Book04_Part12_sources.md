## D. Star reasoning

- Three stars (Q1-Q5): learning-journey openers, the HR-switcher framing and the project walk-through are standard in entry-level interviews and mirror Book 1 Part 12 and Book 2 Part 12; Power BI is named in roughly a third to 40 percent of sampled JDs and Power BI/DAX/project topics appear in the Deloitte, Wipro and Coursera material. This is judgement, not a measured frequency.
- Two stars (Q6-Q20): practice, tools, resources, datasets, licence and version, Mac, verification, live-test preparation, AI use. Licence and Mac questions are practical for HR switchers (free vs Pro limits, Windows-only Desktop) and verified against Microsoft pages.
- One star (Q21-Q27): certifications (none required in any sampled JD), public portfolio, PL-300 content, self-check test, plan, coaching: differentiators.

## E. Limitations and honesty notes

- No source was found that reports "how did you learn Power BI" style questions at a named company. All learning-journey questions are standard-opener inferences, as in the Book 1 and Book 2 Part 12 lists. Of 27 questions, 8 touch source evidence (Q4, Q8, Q9, Q10, Q11, Q16, Q21, Q23); 19 are gap-fills.
- Sample sizes: interview evidence is guide-level and snippet-level (about 10 vendor guides seen as snippets, 1 fetched). Microsoft behaviour and price pages were fetched in full.
- Prices change: Pro and PPU prices above are the pricing page's marketing prices on the fetch date, not quotes. The India figures came from a fetch summary, not a quoted line; re-check before putting them in an answer.
- Not checked: any YouTube channel, Coursera/Udemy/LinkedIn Learning course content, Kaggle datasets, Tableau Public. Glassdoor, AmbitionBox, Reddit not fetched. Microsoft Learn free-ness of training modules is not stated on fetched pages and is unverified; the Power BI downloads page returned 403 and two Learn training URLs returned 404.
- Search results include course ads and spam pages; none were used.
