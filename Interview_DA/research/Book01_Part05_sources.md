- Three stars: Remove Duplicates, TRIM, duplicate detection, Text to Columns, Flash Fill and the "how do you clean a messy dataset" opener appear in nearly every fetched or snippet-level Excel list, and Excel is a must-have in 60% of JDs. The three yellow 3-star items are the standard follow-ups to those (what Excel keeps, trimming a column, safe backup).
- Two stars: standard follow-ups and common practical tasks (conditional formatting for duplicates, CLEAN, PROPER, text-as-number, splitting/joining names, blanks, data validation, hidden-space and date-as-text troubleshooting) seen in some sources or standard for MIS roles; "latest record per ID" and COUNTIF flagging are typical MIS scenarios not seen in a source.
- One star: differentiators (Google Sheets, UNIQUE trade-offs, TEXTSPLIT, Power Query, fuzzy near-duplicates, performance, regex-free extraction).
