# Cross-part duplicate resolution (Book 11, Wave A)

Method: token-overlap scan (Jaccard >= 0.55) of all 699 Book 11 questions within the book and against every question in `question_index.md` (Books 0-10), then manual judgement. The generic "tell me about a time..." prompts overlapped between Part 3 (generic STAR bank) and Parts 5, 6 (teamwork, ownership); each was kept in the part that owns the angle and removed from the part with surplus questions. Kept on purpose (different angle): B11P1Q1/Q15/Q40 (tell me about yourself variants), B11P3Q13 and Q29 vs B11P5Q12 (explain vs persuade, rule vs persuade), B11P3Q32 vs B11P6Q35, B11P3Q39 vs B11P6Q2 (own-error vs shared-error), B11P3Q73 vs B11P5Q45, B11P4Q50 (persuade using data), B11P6Q19 vs B11P10Q2, the Book 0 strategy FAQs vs the interview-room wording (B0Q58, B0Q18, B0Q66), the per-tool "keep skills current" questions in Books 3, 5, 7, the Part 10 and Book 8/10 30-day-plan questions. Old IDs are pre-removal numbers.
