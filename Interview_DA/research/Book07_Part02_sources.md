## Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured)
Evidence says guesstimates are common at Mu Sigma, Fractal, Flipkart and Meesho and occasional at Tiger Analytics, but no source gives frequencies per question. Stars are therefore judgement: 3 stars for the named, repeatedly reported classics (chai, cars, coffee shops, petrol stations, smartphones, grocery delivery, chicken, stores in a city) plus the method questions every guesstimate needs (clarify, top-down vs bottom-up, sanity check, reconcile) and basic exercises with inline numbers; 2 stars for common variants, HR/people markets and mid-difficulty startup/fintech markets; 1 star for differentiators (cattle trading, pet food, unusual product choice, reconciliation with a client). Roughly 55% of entries are fully specified estimation prompts or numeric exercises with inline assumed inputs; the rest are method questions or HR/startup framings. No real-world statistic is stated as fact in any question; numeric exercises are labelled as exercise inputs. Gap-fill questions not directly traced to a source (about 40, added for coverage of HR, SaaS, edtech, fintech, EV and quick-commerce contexts) are judgement, not reported interview questions. Unverified: that any listed question was asked at a named company in 2025-26; the Dataford/Glassdoor company attributions come from search summaries only.
