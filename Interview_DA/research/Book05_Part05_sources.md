Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured frequency)
- 3 stars: population/sample, parameter/statistic, bias, CLT, CI meaning and calculation, SD vs SE. These recur in every prep source and are the baseline for any "statistics" box on a JD.
- 2 stars: named sampling methods, specific bias types, CI misinterpretation, sample-size and survey-response scenarios, HR-flavoured cases. Common in lists, less certain in a single DA loop.
- 1 star: bootstrapping, prediction interval, FPC, overlapping CIs, weighting; differentiators for analytics firms only.

Limitations
- All evidence is generic prep material; no source gives an interview frequency. Company attributions (Wells Fargo, Meta, Tiger) come from search snippets and are unverified.
- 18 of 53 questions are gap-fills from standard practice (calculation tasks, HR scenarios, misconception questions, difference questions) rather than directly seen in a source: the "Calculate..." items, survey 300-of-1,000, exit interviews, invoice audit, sample-size for +/-3%, poll lead, t vs z, with vs without replacement, quota vs stratified.
