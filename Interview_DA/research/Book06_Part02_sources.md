Star-assignment reasoning (judgement, not measured frequency)
- 3 stars: ratio sharing, ratio chaining, simple and weighted averages, replacement/inclusion averages, ages, basic alligation and mixture, simple partnership. These recur in every prep source and in company-tagged banks for TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, Cognizant.

- 1 star: multi-step replacement, partnership with withdrawal, cumulative averages, discard problems; occasional differentiators.

Limitations

- Most of the 67 questions are gap-fills written from standard patterns (classic Indian aptitude question types); only the age-ratio pattern, 7:5 headcount, alligation/mixture concepts, replacement/water-addition and the ratio/average topics are directly signalled by snippets.
