### Q62 ⭐⭐ 🟡 A hiring funnel starts with 2,000 applicants. 40% are shortlisted, 25% of the shortlisted receive offers, and 80% of the offered candidates join. How many join?
**Asked at:** commonly asked (no company-specific report found); HR funnel framing is an original business-flavour item, not taken from a source.
**What the interviewer is checking:** Chained percentage of a funnel.
**Say this in the interview:** "2,000 x 40% = 800 shortlisted; 25% get offers = 200; 80% join = 160."
**Answer:** **160 candidates join**
**Step-by-step working:**
1. Shortlisted = 40% of 2,000 = 800.
2. Offers = 25% of 800 = 200.
3. Joiners = 80% of 200 = 160.
**Fast method (30 seconds):** Chain the fractions: 2/5 x 1/4 x 4/5 = 8/100 = 8%; 8% of 2,000 = 160.
**Check:** Exact: 2000 x 0.4 x 0.25 x 0.8 = 160 (script). That is an overall 8% conversion.
**Explain simply:**
- Each stage's percent applies to the people left from the previous stage, not to the original 2,000.
- The overall conversion is the product of the stage conversions: 0.4 x 0.25 x 0.8 = 8%.
**Likely follow-ups:**
- What if joining is 60% rather than 80%? 120 join.
- How many applicants must we receive to get 100 joiners at 8%? 1,250.
**Common mistakes / traps:** Adding the percentages or applying all three to 2,000 each.
